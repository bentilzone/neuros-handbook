### Platform operator
The team that runs Neuros itself. Its staff act through platform roles, not as a company. They see each company's counts and administrators, but not its business, without a support grant.

### Application
A business's request to join Neuros. It is filled in, documents are uploaded, and it is submitted.
1. **Submitted**
2. **Under review**
3. **Resubmission requested**, with reasons, when something must be corrected.
4. **Approved**, with the capabilities granted, or **Rejected**.

### Company status
- **Active**
- **Suspended:** nobody can trade as it until it is reactivated.
- **Closed**

### Support grant (support access)
Time-limited, read-only entry into one company for Neuros support staff, for a stated purpose. The **company** grants it, never the operator. Support asks; the company's system administrator is emailed and grants or declines it under **Settings → Support access**. The company can end it at any time; otherwise it expires when the time asked for runs out. While it is active, a banner shows the session is acting under the grant. Every page support opens is recorded in the company's audit log.

### Platform roles
The operator's own roles, such as administrator, reviewer and support, each with platform permissions.

### Scheduler
Neuros's background jobs: renewals, reminders, expiring grants, cleaning old history. The operator can see when each last ran, run it now, pause it or change how often it runs.

### Notification
A note to one person that something concerning them happened in their company: an order arrived, a payment was confirmed, a change waits for an answer. It goes to the people who can act on it or who started it, never to the person who did the thing. It arrives as it happens, under the bell at the top right, and some also come by email. Each person reads and clears their own.

### Action item
A notification that needs someone to do something, such as accept an order or confirm a payment. The bell turns orange while you have one unread, and a short note appears when one arrives.
