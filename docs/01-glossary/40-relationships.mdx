### Account (trading relationship)
The standing arrangement between a seller and a buyer: the buyer's account with that seller. It carries the terms they trade on. Both sides see it: the seller under **Customers**, the buyer under **Suppliers**.

### Account status
- **Waiting for approval:** the buyer asked (or the seller invited) and the seller hasn't answered.
- **Approved:** they trade on the agreed terms.
- **Declined:** the seller said no.
- **Suspended:** the seller paused trading, with a reason; it can be reinstated.
- **Ended:** closed for good. Either side may end it.

### Terms
What an approved account trades on, set by the seller:
- the price list the buyer gets;
- the payment modes allowed and the default one, with payment days;
- whether the buyer may buy on credit, and up to how much;
- the largest single order;
- the dates the terms apply between;
- the seller's private notes about the customer.

### Credit limit
The most a credit-eligible buyer may owe a seller at once, in the seller's own currency. The seller sets it, optionally with an expiry date.

### Credit in use (exposure)
The part of the limit already claimed: orders on credit that the seller accepted and the buyer has not yet paid. Each order claims its value when it is accepted and gives it back when it is paid or cancelled. **Available** is the limit less what is in use.

### Credit hold
A pause the seller puts on an account, with a reason: no new orders on credit until it is lifted. Open orders and invoices are untouched.

### Credit override
A one-off approval to go over a buyer's limit by a set amount, with a reason and an expiry. The next order that needs it uses it, once. Only the seller sees overrides.

### Payment mode
How a buyer may pay on an account: pay before dispatch, bank transfer, on credit, deposit with the balance later, milestones, or escrow.

### Supplier code
The buyer's own reference for a seller, such as their supplier number in their own system. Only the buyer sees it.

### Seller directory
The list of selling companies a buyer can ask for an account with, under **Suppliers → Find sellers**.
