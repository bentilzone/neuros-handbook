### Plan (package)
What a company subscribes to. It sets the limits (members, warehouses, API clients, published offers, custom roles), how long history is kept and which paid features are on. It is priced per month or year in several currencies. A plan never grants a capability.

### Add-on
An extra on top of a plan: more of a limit (ten more members, one more warehouse), longer history, or a feature such as webhooks. Add-ons stack.

### Not in your plan
A screen whose feature (Webhooks, Bulk import) your plan lacks. It shows a padlock in the sidebar and opens read-only: what you set up before stays readable, and the actions that need the feature are off. Nothing is deleted when a feature goes.

### Limit
The most of something a plan allows. When it is reached, Neuros refuses the next one and names the add-on that would lift it. Going over after a downgrade never deletes anything; only new ones are refused.

### Trial
A free start on a plan. Each company gets one trial; a company that has trialled before is billed straight away.

### Subscription status
- **Trial**
- **Active**
- **Past due:** an invoice is unpaid.
- **Lapsed:** read-only. You can see everything but change nothing until it is paid.
- **Cancelled:** runs to the end of the period.
- **Ended**

### No plan
A company without a live subscription can only sign in, see its own company details and choose a plan. Members without billing rights are told to ask their administrator.

### Invoice, receipt and credit note
The billing documents Neuros sends the company for its subscription, each with a PDF. A receipt records a payment against an invoice. A credit note reduces one.

### Usage
What a company used in a month, counted by meter: orders, active resellers, agent tool calls, messages by kind, escrow releases, order value and fees. Each order, message or release is counted once, when it happens. Neuros corrects usage only with an **adjustment**: a separate record with its reason.

### Rate card
What usage costs on a plan, in one currency. For each meter: an **allowance** included each month, then a price for each one over it, in steps if the price falls. It also sets the transaction fee and any monthly minimum. Neuros can give one company its own rate card.

### Allowance
How many of a meter a plan includes each month at no charge. The company is told at 80% and at 100%.

### Transaction fee
What Neuros charges on an accepted order not paid through escrow: a percent of the order plus a flat amount, between a minimum and a maximum. Escrow orders pay the bank's fee at release instead.

### Usage statement and usage invoice
At the end of each month, Neuros writes each company's statement: every meter with what was used, included and charged. If anything is owed, a usage invoice follows, taxed and paid like any other.

### Books
Neuros' own accounting, kept outside Neuros in Zoho Billing. Every invoice and payment is copied there; Neuros' invoice is the one that counts.
