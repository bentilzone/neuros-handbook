### Plan (package)
What a company subscribes to. It sets the limits (members, warehouses, API clients, published offers, custom roles), how long history is kept and which paid features are on. It is priced per month or year in several currencies. A plan never grants a capability.

### Add-on
An extra on top of a plan: more of a limit (ten more members, one more warehouse), longer history, or a feature such as webhooks. Add-ons stack.

### Limit
The most of something a plan allows. When it is reached, Neuros refuses the next one and names the add-on that would lift it. Going over after a downgrade never deletes anything; only new ones are refused.

### Trial
A free start on a plan. Each company gets one trial; a company that has trialled before is billed straight away.

### Subscription status
- **Trial**
- **Active**
- **Past due:** an invoice is unpaid.
- **Lapsed:** read-only. You can see everything but change nothing until it is paid.
- **Cancelled:** runs to the end of the period.
- **Ended**

### No plan
A company without a live subscription can only sign in, see its own company details and choose a plan. Members without billing rights are told to ask their administrator.

### Invoice, receipt and credit note
The billing documents Neuros sends the company for its subscription, each with a PDF. A receipt records a payment against an invoice. A credit note reduces one.
