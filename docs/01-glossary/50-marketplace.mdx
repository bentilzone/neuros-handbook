### Marketplace
Where buyers find offers from every seller they can buy from, side by side, for the same catalogue products.

### Listing
What a buyer sees of a published offer: the product, the seller, the price where they may see it, the availability and the ordering rules. It never includes the seller's costs, other customers' prices or exact stock unless the seller chose to show it.

### Availability
What buyers see instead of a stock count:
- **In stock**
- **Low stock**
- **Out of stock**
- **On backorder**
- **Made to order**

A seller may opt to show the actual quantity.

### Your price
The price this buyer would pay on an offer, from the pricing precedence: their contract price, their price list or the standard price, with any promotion. A buyer can check it for any quantity, in any of the product's units. It shows where it came from, for example *Your price list*.

### Filters (facets)
The panels beside the results: availability, category, brand and seller. Each shows how many offers the filter would leave.
