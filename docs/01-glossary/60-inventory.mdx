### Warehouse
A place you hold stock: a depot, a shop, a site store. Each has a code (`MAIN`) and a name. One is your default. The number of warehouses is a plan limit.

### Zone and bin
Optional places inside a warehouse: zones (an aisle, a cold room) and bins within them. Without them, stock is held at warehouse level.

### Stock on hand
How much of a product is physically in a warehouse, in each condition.

### Reserved
Stock held for an order that hasn't left yet. It is still on hand but can't be sold again.

### Free to sell (available)
Sellable stock on hand minus what is reserved. It is the only stock an order can claim, and two orders can never claim the same last unit.

### Condition
The state of stock, which decides whether it can be sold:
- **Sellable**
- **Quarantine:** held back for checking.
- **Damaged**
- **Expired**

Moving stock between conditions is a *condition change*. Releasing quarantined stock makes it sellable again.

### Receipt (receive stock)
Recording stock that arrived: opening stock, a delivery without a purchase order. Each line has a product, a warehouse, a quantity and a unit cost; lot or serial numbers are added for tracked products. A receipt is sent exactly once, however often the button is pressed.

### Movement
One line in the stock history: received, issued, adjusted in or out, transferred, counted, quarantined, released, written off. Movements are never edited or deleted. Stock changes only by writing one.

### Adjustment
A correction to stock with a reason (damage, theft, found stock). It is proposed and changes nothing until someone approves it. With segregation of duties on, that can't be the proposer.

### Transfer
Moving stock between your warehouses:
1. **Draft**
2. **In transit:** it has left the first warehouse and is in neither until received.
3. **Received**, or **Received short:** the missing quantity is written off as lost in transit.

A draft can be cancelled.

### Stock count
Counting what is actually on the shelf:
1. **Counting:** you record what you found.
2. **Waiting for approval:** submitted.
3. **Approved:** the differences (count gains and losses) are applied.

### Reorder rule
For a product in a warehouse: the level at which to reorder and how much. Neuros suggests what is at or below its level, but never orders by itself.

### Valuation
What your stock is worth at cost, in your functional currency, by your company's method:
- **FIFO:** the oldest cost is used first.
- **Weighted average:** one running average cost.

The method can't be changed once stock has been valued. The total always equals the inventory account in your ledger.
