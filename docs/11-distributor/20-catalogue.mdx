The catalogue is Neuros' shared list of real products, one record per product, used by every seller. Prices and stock are not here. They live on each seller's [offer](./30-offers.mdx). Two distributors selling the same pump both point at the same catalogue product, which is how a buyer can compare them side by side in the [marketplace](./60-marketplace.mdx).

<Shot id="distributor/catalogue/browse" />

## Browse

- **Categories** on the left narrow the list to one branch of the tree.
- **Search** matches product name, brand and part number. **Type** filters by product type.
- **Click a row** to open the product: its category, brand, part number, GTIN, attributes, units and history.

## My contributions

When a product you sell is missing, you contribute it. **My contributions** lists the products you have submitted and where each one stands.

<Shot id="distributor/catalogue/mine" />

| Status | What it means | What moves it |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Saved, not yet sent | You submit it (send icon on the row) |
| In review | Waiting for Neuros | Neuros publishes it or rejects it with a reason |
| Rejected | Sent back, with the reviewer's reason | You edit it (pencil on the row) and submit again |
| Published | In the shared catalogue | Every seller can now offer it |

Neuros reviews each product before it is published. The catalogue stays one record per real product, so a duplicate or a vague name is sent back.

## Common questions

**Can I edit a product after it is published?** No. You can edit your contribution only while it is a draft or after it is rejected. Once published it belongs to the shared catalogue, so every seller's offers stay accurate. Ask Neuros support for a correction.

**Can I delete a product?** No. Other sellers may already offer it. Withdraw your own [offer](./30-offers.mdx) instead.
