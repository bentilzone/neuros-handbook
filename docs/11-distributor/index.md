You buy from your suppliers, hold stock and sell on to your customers. So you see every workspace page: selling, buying, inventory and the ledger.

This guide walks the app as **Lagos Industrial Supplies Ltd**, a demo distributor in Nigeria whose books are in naira. It follows the sidebar from top to bottom:

| Section | Pages |
|---|---|
| Main | [Dashboard](./10-dashboard.mdx), [Catalogue](./20-catalogue.mdx) |
| Sell | [Offers](./30-offers.mdx), [Pricing](./40-pricing.mdx), [Quotes](./55-quotes.mdx), [Orders](./57-orders.mdx), [Disputes and returns](./59-disputes.mdx), [Invoices](./58-invoices.mdx), [Customers](./50-customers.mdx) |
| Buy | [Marketplace](./60-marketplace.mdx), [Quotes](./75-buying-quotes.mdx), [Basket](./76-basket.mdx), [Orders](./77-buying-orders.mdx), [Disputes and returns](./79-buying-disputes.mdx), [Invoices](./78-buying-invoices.mdx), [Suppliers](./70-suppliers.mdx) |
| Stock & finance | [Inventory](./80-inventory.mdx), [Fulfilment](./85-fulfilment.mdx), [Ledger](./90-ledger.mdx) |
| System | [Notifications](./95-notifications.mdx), [Members](./100-members.mdx), [Roles](./110-roles.mdx), [API clients](./120-api-clients.mdx), [Audit log](./130-audit-log.mdx) |
| You | [Settings](./140-settings.mdx), every tab |

Each page says who sees it, shows it with numbered callouts, and explains every field of every form: what it means to you, what the other company sees, and what it commits you to.

:::tip[Your currency]
Every amount you enter defaults to your company's functional currency, here NGN. You only see another currency when you choose one, for example on a price list for buyers abroad, or when a supplier prices in theirs.
:::
