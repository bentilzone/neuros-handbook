Inventory is your stock on hand in each warehouse, and every receipt, adjustment, transfer and count that changes it. Every change is a movement that is never edited or deleted, and each one that has a value posts to your [ledger](./90-ledger.mdx). See [inventory](../01-glossary/60-inventory.mdx) and [stock, end to end](../02-features/70-stock.mdx).

The tabs update on their own: a colleague's receipt or approval appears as it happens.

## Stock

<Shot id="distributor/inventory/stock" />

One row per product, warehouse, condition and lot:

- **On hand** is what is physically there.
- **Reserved** is held for orders.
- **Free to sell** is on hand minus reserved. Offers sold from stock can sell only this.

Only **Sellable** stock is free to sell. Quarantined, damaged and expired stock stays on hand but cannot be sold.

## Movements

<Shot id="distributor/inventory/movements" />

Every change to stock, newest first: receipts, condition changes, adjustments, transfers and count variances, each with its quantity and value. Filter by warehouse, type and date. Movements are never edited. A mistake is corrected by a new movement, such as an adjustment.

## Adjustments

<Shot id="distributor/inventory/adjustments" />

An adjustment corrects stock: a breakage, a write-off, stock found. Nothing moves until someone with approval rights applies it. With segregation of duties on (Settings → My company → Operating policies), that cannot be the person who proposed it.

<Shot id="distributor/inventory/adjustment-new" />

| Status | What moves it |
|---|---|
| Waiting for approval | **Approve and apply** moves the stock; **Reject** asks for a note, and the proposer sees your reason. No stock moves |
| Applied | Done |
| Rejected | Done; nothing moved |

## Transfers

<Shot id="distributor/inventory/transfers" />

A transfer moves stock between two of your warehouses. You need at least two active warehouses; with one, **New transfer** stays disabled.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Draft | Prepared; nothing has moved. **Cancel transfer** closes it |
| In transit | **Dispatched**: the stock has left the source |
| Received | Arrived in full at the destination |
| Received short | Less arrived than was sent: the difference is written off as lost in transit |
| Cancelled | Closed as a draft; nothing moved |

## Counts

<Shot id="distributor/inventory/counts" />

A count checks what is on the shelf against what Neuros holds.

<Shot id="distributor/inventory/count-open" />

1. **Open a count** for a warehouse. It snapshots the system quantity of every stock pool there.
2. **Record what you counted** line by line, adding lines for stock you found. **Save counts** as you go.
3. **Submit** once every line is counted.
4. Someone with count approval rights **approves** it. Each variance against the snapshot becomes a movement.

**Cancel count** discards the recorded counts; no stock moves.

## Valuation

<Shot id="distributor/inventory/valuation" />

What your stock is worth, product by product, in your company currency. It uses your valuation method: weighted average or FIFO, set under Settings → My company → Operating policies. The total is the same figure your Inventory account shows in the ledger.
