Members are the people who work in your company: what each one may do, and how much they may spend or approve. See [companies and people](../01-glossary/10-companies-and-people.mdx) and [people and access](../02-features/20-people-and-access.mdx).

<Shot id="distributor/members/list" />

:::note[Two-step sign-in needed]
Inviting people, or changing their roles and limits, needs a session signed in with two-step verification. Turn it on under [Settings → Security](./140-settings.mdx#security), then sign in again with your code. Until then the banner shows and the buttons stay disabled.
:::

The **Members** tab lists everyone with access. **Invitations** lists the emails you have invited and whether each is still pending. The **Access** switch turns a member's access off without removing them, and back on.

## A member

Click a row for the member: roles, limits, two-step verification, phone, when they joined and last signed in, and the history of changes to their access.

<Shot id="distributor/members/detail" />

**Edit** changes their title, roles and limits. It asks for a **reason for the change**, which is kept with the change in the audit log. **Remove** takes them out of the company; their past actions stay in the history.
