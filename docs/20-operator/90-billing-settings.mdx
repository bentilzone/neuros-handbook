How Neuros bills: who the invoices are from, terms, grace, trials, reminders, how companies pay, numbering and tax. **Save changes** asks for a reason, kept in the audit log, and needs a session signed in with two-step verification.

## Payment details

<Shot id="operator/billing-settings/payment" />

**How companies pay**: the bank accounts printed on every unpaid invoice and shown in each company's Settings → Billing. **Add a bank account** for each currency you take.

<FieldTable fields={[
  { name: 'Bank, Account name, Account number', required: true, meaning: 'Where companies send money.', others: 'Printed on invoices.' },
  { name: 'Branch, SWIFT', meaning: 'For transfers that need them.' },
  { name: 'For payments in', meaning: 'The currency this account takes.', rules: 'Blank: any currency.' },
  { name: 'Instructions', meaning: 'Anything the payer must do, such as quote the invoice number.' },
]} />

This tab also sets the billing email, the footer printed on invoices, and the prefixes that number invoices, credit notes and payments.

## Tax

<Shot id="operator/billing-settings/tax" />

The tax charged on subscriptions, by the buyer's country. A company in a country with no rate is invoiced without tax.
