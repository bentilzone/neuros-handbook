What Neuros has billed, collected and is owed. Invoices, receipts and credit notes are Neuros' own documents. Its books are kept outside Neuros and fed by the **Reports** tab. See [plans and billing](../02-features/90-plans-and-billing.mdx).

## Overview

<Shot id="operator/billing/overview" />

Monthly recurring revenue, what is outstanding and overdue, trials ending this week, what was billed and collected this month, how many companies pay, and how many are cancelling. Amounts are shown per currency: each company is billed in its own.

## Invoices

<Shot id="operator/billing/invoices" />

Subscriptions raise their invoices on their own, one per period. Filter by status, or **Overdue only**.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Open | Issued, nothing paid yet |
| Partially paid | Some paid, the rest still owed |
| Paid | Settled |
| Void | Cancelled with a credit note for the whole of it; its number stays used |

### An invoice

<Shot id="operator/billing/invoice" />

Click an invoice for its lines, tax, payments and credit notes, and the PDF to view or download.

- **Record payment** records money received against it.
- **Send reminder** emails the company's billing contact now.
- **Credit note** reduces what is owed. Leave the amount blank to credit the whole balance.
- **Void** cancels an unpaid invoice with a credit note for the whole of it.

## Reports

<Shot id="operator/billing/reports" />

- **Revenue by month**: invoiced, earned, tax charged and collected, per currency. A subscription invoice is earned month by month over the period it covers. What is still to be earned after the range is shown below it.
- **Receivables**: what each company owes, by how late it is.
- **Export journal (CSV)**: the period as journal lines for Neuros' own accounting system.
