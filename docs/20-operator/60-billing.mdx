What Neuros has billed, collected and is owed. Invoices, receipts and credit notes are Neuros' own documents. Its books are kept outside Neuros and fed by the **Reports** tab. See [plans and billing](../02-features/90-plans-and-billing.mdx).

## Overview

<Shot id="operator/billing/overview" />

Monthly recurring revenue, what is outstanding and overdue, trials ending this week, what was billed and collected this month, how many companies pay, and how many are cancelling. Amounts are shown per currency: each company is billed in its own.

Below the figures, **Books** says whether Zoho Billing is connected and how many invoices and payments have not reached it.
- **Connected:** every invoice and payment is copied there as it is made. A failed copy is tried again, up to 10 times.
- **Not connected:** they wait, and go across once the server has the Zoho Billing settings.

## Invoices

<Shot id="operator/billing/invoices" />

Subscriptions raise their invoices on their own, one per period. Filter by status, or **Overdue only**.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Open | Issued, nothing paid yet |
| Partially paid | Some paid, the rest still owed |
| Paid | Settled |
| Void | Cancelled with a credit note for the whole of it; its number stays used |

### An invoice

<Shot id="operator/billing/invoice" />

Click an invoice for its lines, tax, payments and credit notes, and the PDF to view or download.

- **Record payment** records money received against it.
- **Send reminder** emails the company's billing contact now.
- **Credit note** reduces what is owed. Leave the amount blank to credit the whole balance.
- **Void** cancels an unpaid invoice with a credit note for the whole of it.
- **Zoho Billing** shows its copy in the books: **In Zoho**, **Waiting** or **Failed** with the reason. **Send again** retries one that failed. Zoho finds an earlier copy by the invoice number, so it is never copied twice.

A usage invoice (kind **Usage**) is issued when a month closes; see [Usage](#usage) below.

### A payment

<Shot id="operator/billing/payment" />

The payment, its receipt, and the invoices it was applied to. A payment goes to Zoho Billing once the invoices it paid are there, with **Send again** if it failed. A payment taken in Zoho comes back on its own: it is recorded once, on the invoices it paid, with Zoho's id as its reference.

## Usage

<Shot id="operator/billing/usage" />

Each company's usage in a month, at its rates: its own rates, its plan's, or none. Pick the month; this month is rated as it stands. See [usage](../02-features/90-plans-and-billing.mdx#usage).

When a month ends, Neuros closes it within the hour, once for each subscription:
- a statement with each meter;
- if anything is owed and the company is not on trial, a usage invoice.

### A company's usage

<Shot id="operator/billing/usage-company" />

- **Meters**: what was used, included and charged, with the allowances.
- **Records**: each record and what it came from (an order, a message, a release, the month's close), and adjustments with their reason.
- **Statements**: the months closed, with their usage invoice.

## Reports

<Shot id="operator/billing/reports" />

- **Revenue by month**: invoiced, earned, tax charged and collected, per currency. A subscription invoice is earned month by month over the period it covers. What is still to be earned after the range is shown below it.
- **Receivables**: what each company owes, by how late it is.
- **Export journal (CSV)**: the period as journal lines for Neuros' own accounting system.
