What Neuros sells. A plan sets limits, retention and features at a price per currency; add-ons raise one of them. Changes reach subscribers at their next renewal: until then they keep what they bought. See [plans and billing](../01-glossary/80-plans-and-billing.mdx).

## Plans

<Shot id="operator/plans/plans" />

Each plan shows its prices, how many companies hold it, and what it includes. **Edit** changes it; archiving one stops new companies taking it, and companies that hold it keep it until they change.
