Which plan each company holds, what it pays and what happens next. A company holds one subscription. See [plans and billing](../01-glossary/80-plans-and-billing.mdx).

<Shot id="operator/subscriptions/list" />

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Trialing | On a free trial; billing starts when it ends |
| Active | Paid up |
| Past due | An invoice is overdue, within the grace period |
| Lapsed | Past the grace period: the company is read-only until it pays |
| Cancelled | Not renewing; it keeps access until the period ends |
| Ended | Over |

## A subscription

<Shot id="operator/subscriptions/detail" />

Click a row for the subscription, its invoices, what it includes and what the company uses of it.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Change plan** | A dearer plan applies now, with a pro-rata invoice. A cheaper plan or a new cycle applies at renewal. |
| **Add-ons** | Added units apply now, charged pro rata. Removed units stay until renewal. |
| **Extend trial** / **End trial and bill** | More trial days, or billing from today with the first invoice issued at once. |
| **Extend grace** | More time before an unpaid company becomes read-only. |
| **Free periods** | Bill the next periods at 100% off. |
| **Custom price** | A negotiated price from the next invoice. Clear it to return to the plan's price. |
| **Cancel** / **Resume** | Stop renewing (access lasts until the period ends) or end now. Resume undoes a cancellation before the period ends. |
