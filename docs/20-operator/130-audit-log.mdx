Who did what on the platform: sign-ins, onboarding, companies, the catalogue, support access, billing and staff. A company's own changes stay in its own log. See [audit trail](../01-glossary/90-platform.mdx).

<Shot id="operator/audit/log" />

- **Changes** lists every change. **API requests** lists the requests themselves: who called what, and when.
- **The cards** count events by record type. Click one to filter.
- **Filter** by action, record and time.
- How long each kind is kept is shown at the top right.

## An event

Click an event to see what changed, field by field, and the reason when one was given.

<Shot id="operator/audit/event" />

Nobody can edit or delete an event. Retention removes old ones on schedule, and that removal is audited too.
