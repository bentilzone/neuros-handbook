The roles every company starts with, and the roles platform staff hold. A change reaches everyone holding the role straight away. See [people and access](../02-features/20-people-and-access.mdx).

## Company roles

<Shot id="operator/roles/organization" />

Each role shows its permissions, how many companies use it, and when it last changed. Companies can't edit these roles; they make custom roles of their own.

Click a role for its permissions. The company administrator role is locked: it always holds every permission, so no edit can lock a company out. Only its name and description change.

<Shot id="operator/roles/detail" />

**Archive** removes a role from every company. Only a role nobody holds can be archived.

## Platform roles

<Shot id="operator/roles/platform" />

| Role | What it allows |
|---|---|
| Super administrator | Every platform power: companies, staff, reference data, catalogue, support and audit. Locked, like the company administrator. |
| Onboarding reviewer | Reviews company applications and the shared catalogue |
| Support agent | Requests time-boxed, read-only access to a company to help it |
| Platform finance | Subscriptions, invoices, payments, fees, disputes and the platform audit trail |

Archiving a platform role means staff can no longer be given it.
