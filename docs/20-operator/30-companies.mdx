## A company at a glance

Click a row for its system administrator, what Neuros set (legal name, country, functional currency, timezone, capabilities), how long its audit trail is kept, and its subscription. A company with no plan is locked until it subscribes.

## A company's page

**Explore** opens the company's own page.

<Shot id="operator/companies/page" />

| Section | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Set by Neuros | **Edit** what Neuros verified: slug, legal name, registration number, country, functional currency and timezone. The functional currency locks once its books or stock values hold amounts; the timezone once journals are posted, because moving it would re-date their periods. The company sees these but cannot change them, except its slug. |
| Capabilities | **Edit** supply, distribution and purchasing. Grant only what you have verified. |
| System administrator | **Send password reset** emails them a link to choose a new password; Neuros never sees it. **Change system administrator** hands the role to another of the company's administrators. The previous one stays an ordinary administrator. |
| Roles | The company's roles and how many members hold each. **Give a role** creates a role for this company only, which its administrators then manage. |
| Subscription | Its plan, what it owes, what is overdue, its credit, billing contact, limits against use and recent invoices. **Subscribe** puts a company with no plan on one. |
| Audit retention | How long its change history and API request log are kept. Empty follows the platform's period. Never below the platform minimum. |

At the top, **Request support access** asks the company to let you in ([Support access](./50-support-access.mdx)). **Suspend** stops its members working on their next request; nothing is deleted, and **Reactivate** restores it.

Every change on this page asks for a reason, kept in the audit log, and needs a session signed in with two-step verification.
