Neuros staff have no standing access to a company. To help one, you ask; the company decides. Access is read-only, for a set time, and the company's audit log shows every page you open. See [support access](../02-features/95-support-access.mdx).

<Shot id="operator/support/list" />

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Waiting for the company | Asked; the company has not answered |
| Active | Granted. **Open company** takes the requester into it, read-only, until the time runs out |
| Declined | The company said no, or a colleague turned the request down |
| Ended early | **End access now** stopped it before its time, or the requester withdrew it |
| Expired | Its time ran out |

**Whose** shows everyone's requests or only yours. Only the person who asked can open the company. **Withdraw request** takes back your own request before the company answers. A colleague with `platform:support:approve` can **Turn down** someone else's request first, saying why, so they can ask again properly.

Open a request for its trail: sessions started, requests made inside the company (and any refused), first and last activity, and the screens opened most.
