A business that wants to trade on Neuros applies with its details, what it intends to do, and its documents. Applications is where reviewers decide. See [joining Neuros](../02-features/10-joining-neuros.mdx).

<Shot id="operator/applications/list" />

## States

| State | What it means | What moves it |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting for review | Submitted, nobody has it yet | **Start review** |
| In review | A reviewer has it | **Approve**, **Ask for changes** or **Reject** |
| Changes requested | Back with the applicant, with what to fix | The applicant resubmits |
| Approved | The company exists on Neuros | — |
| Declined | Rejected, with the reason | — |

## Review an application

Click a row to read it: the applicant, when it was submitted, what it asked to do, its website, addresses, contacts and documents, and its history.

<Shot id="operator/applications/review" />

- **Start review** takes the application, so colleagues can see someone has it.
- **Documents** open in a new tab. Each view is recorded in the audit log.
- **Ask for changes** sends it back with a note saying what to fix.
- **Reject** closes it, with a note saying why.

## Approve

Approving creates the company's workspace and its standard roles, and makes the applicant its system administrator, all in one step. The administrator can sign in at once.
