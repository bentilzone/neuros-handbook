The console has its own notifications, for platform staff. They tell you about work waiting for you, and answer requests you made. Each member of staff sees only their own. See [notification](../01-glossary/90-platform.mdx#notification).

<Shot id="operator/notifications/list" />

The bell at the top right counts what is unread and opens the latest beside the page; **See all** opens this page. Click one to open what it is about, which also marks it read.

| When | Who is told |
|---|---|
| A company submits its application | Staff who review applications |
| A company proposes a product for the shared catalogue | Staff who manage the catalogue |
| A company grants, declines or ends support access you asked for | You |

You are never told about something you did yourself. Choose what reaches your bell under [Settings → Notifications](./140-settings.mdx#notifications).
