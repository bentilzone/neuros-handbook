This page is for people who deliver for a company on Neuros. You do not need an account, an app or a password. The company sends you a link by message on the morning of the run, and the link opens your day’s deliveries on your phone.

<Shot id="distributor/driver/run" />

## Your stops

The link lists your stops in the order to go: who, where, and what you carry for each. A stop you have finished shows how it ended. Tap a stop to deliver it. **Call** rings the customer.

## Delivering

1. **Hand over the goods.** For each item, the quantity starts at what you loaded. If you hand over less, change it and choose why: **Short**, **Damaged** or **Wrong item**.
2. **Add a photo** of the goods at the door, if the company asks for one.
3. **The place** is recorded if your phone allows it. If it says *No place*, tap **Try again** or deliver without it.
4. **Ask for the code.** The customer has six digits from the company. Ask for them only once the goods are handed over, and enter them.
5. Tap **Delivered**.

A wrong code says how many tries are left. After three wrong codes the stop is locked. Call the company: they can send the customer a new code.

## When you cannot deliver

Tap **Could not deliver** and say what happened: nobody there, refused, the wrong address. The goods go back to the company for another run.

## Keep the link to yourself

Anyone with the link can see your stops. It works until the end of the day. If you lose it, or it reaches someone else, ask the company for a new one: the old one stops at once.
