Neuros is where distributors, suppliers and resellers trade with each other and keep the records that trade creates. That means the catalogue, offers and prices, the accounts companies hold with each other, stock in each warehouse, and the books that follow from all of it.

New here? [Signing in](./20-signing-in.mdx) covers creating an account, two-step verification and choosing your company.

This handbook explains Neuros in three passes:

1. **[Glossary](/glossary).** Every word Neuros uses, defined once. When a page says *offer*, *price list* or *period*, it means exactly what the glossary says.
2. **[Features end to end](/features).** Each feature followed across everyone it touches. For example, a seller publishes an offer, a buyer finds it, asks for an account and sees *their* price.
3. **Guides by role.** Every page of the app in the order you meet it, from the Dashboard to the last Settings tab:
   - [Suppliers](/supplier)
   - [Distributors](/distributor)
   - [Resellers and buyers](/reseller)
   - [the platform operator](/operator)

## How each page is laid out

Every page in the role guides follows the same shape:

- **Who sees it.** The kinds of company and the permission it needs.
- **The screen.** Numbered markers on the screenshot match the numbered list under it.
- **What you can do.** Each action, step by step.
- **The forms.** A table for every form: what each field means to you, what the other party sees because of it, and what it commits you to.
- **States.** What a record can be (draft, published, posted…) and what moves it.
- **Live.** What updates on its own when someone else acts.
