The sidebar lists the pages you may open, grouped as Sell, Buy, Stock & Finance and System. Many pages have tabs: Delivery runs, for example, is a tab of Fulfilment. When you don’t know where something lives, search for it.

## Search

Press **⌘K** on a Mac or **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux, from any page. **/** works too when you are not typing in a field. You can also click **Search…** at the top of the screen.

Type the name of a page, a tab or a report, or the word you would use for it. For example, “dispatch” or “driver” finds Delivery runs, “bank” finds Collections, and “password” finds Settings → Security.

| You find | Example | Where it takes you |
|---|---|---|
| A page | Customers | The page, as from the sidebar |
| A tab | Payments received | The page, open on that tab |
| A report | Receivables ageing | Reports, with that report chosen |

Each result shows where it lives in the sidebar, for example *Stock & Finance › Fulfilment*. This also tells pages with the same name apart, such as Sell › Orders and Buy › Orders.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| ↑ ↓ | Move through the results |
| ↵ | Open the result |
| ⌘↵ or Ctrl+↵ | Open it in a new browser tab |
| esc | Close search |

Before you type, search shows the last five places you opened from it, for you in this company and in this browser.

Search shows only what you may open: the same pages your role and your company’s kind of business put in your sidebar. It finds pages, tabs and reports. To find a particular order, customer or product, open its page and use that page’s own search.

## Lists to choose from

Every list you choose from filters as you type. Click the field and type part of a name to narrow a long list of reports, customers, products or warehouses. **Nothing matches** means no option contains what you typed.
