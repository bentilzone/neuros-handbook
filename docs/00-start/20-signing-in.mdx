One Neuros account works for every company you belong to. You sign in once, confirm it is you if two-step verification is on, then choose which company to work in.

## Signing in

<Shot id="visitor/login" />

Enter the email and password of your account and press **Sign in**. Then, depending on your account:

1. **Two-step verification**, if you turned it on or your company requires it. Platform staff always have this step.
2. **Choose a workspace**, if you belong to more than one company (or to a company and the platform console). With exactly one, Neuros opens it straight away.

Five wrong passwords in a row lock the account for 15 minutes. Wait, or reset the password.

## Two-step verification

<Shot id="visitor/mfa" />

Open your authenticator app and type the six-digit code it shows for Neuros. The last digit submits the code.

- **Use a recovery code** swaps the code boxes for one of the recovery codes you saved when you turned two-step verification on. Each code works once.
- **Cancel** goes back to the email and password.

You turn two-step verification on, and get new recovery codes, under **Settings → Security**. Some actions need a session that went through this step, for example granting roles or approving journals: see [People and access](../02-features/20-people-and-access.mdx).

## Choosing a company

**Choose a workspace** lists each company you are a member of, with your roles in it. Pick one to open it. Each company is a separate workspace: its catalogue, stock and books are its own.

Neuros staff also see **Platform console**. **Sign in as someone else** cancels and goes back to the start.

**No workspace yet** means your account belongs to no company. Ask an administrator to invite you, or [apply to join Neuros](../02-features/10-joining-neuros.mdx) for your business.

### Switching later

The company name at the top of the sidebar is a menu. Open it to move to another of your companies, to a company whose [support access](../02-features/95-support-access.mdx) you hold (staff only, read-only until the time shown), or to the platform console. Switching does not sign you out.

## Creating an account

From the sign-in page, **Create an account**.

Neuros then sends a confirmation link. You can't sign in until you open it. A confirmed account belongs to no company yet: either accept an invitation or apply for your business.

### The confirmation link

Opening the link shows **Email confirmed**, and you can sign in. **That link didn't work** means it was already used, has expired or was copied incompletely.

<Shot id="visitor/resend-verification" />

**Resend confirmation** sends a fresh link. For privacy, Neuros answers the same whether or not the address has an account.

## Forgot your password

<Shot id="visitor/forgot-password" />

From the sign-in page, **Forgot password?**, then enter your email. Neuros sends a link if the address has an account, and says the same either way.

The link opens **Choose a new password**: type it twice (at least 10 characters). Changing it signs you out everywhere else, so anyone holding an old session loses it. The link works once and expires; request another if it does.

## Accepting an invitation

An invitation arrives by email from someone in a company. Its link opens **Join your team on Neuros**:

- **You already have an account** with that email: accept, and the company joins your workspaces.
- **You don't**: the page asks for your **Full name** and a **Password** (at least 10 characters) and creates the account as it accepts. The email is already confirmed by the invitation.

**You're in** confirms it. Sign in and pick the company. An invitation that was revoked or has expired can't be accepted; ask for a new one.

## Common questions

**I belong to two companies. Do I need two accounts?** No. One account, and you choose the company after signing in.

**I lost my phone.** Use a recovery code, then turn two-step verification off and on again under **Settings → Security** to set up the new phone. Without recovery codes, contact Neuros support.

**Why was I signed out?** You changed your password, signed out everywhere, or an administrator suspended your membership.
