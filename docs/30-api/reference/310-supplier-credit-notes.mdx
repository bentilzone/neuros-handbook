### Record a supplier’s credit note against one of its invoices

<span className="nh-method nh-method--post">POST</span> `/v1/supplier-credit-notes`

Against an approved invoice, for up to what it has left to credit, tax included and split in the invoice’s proportion. It comes off what is still owed; on an invoice already paid, the rest is taken back off its payments and held as an advance with the supplier. Posted at the invoice’s rate: Dr accounts payable (and supplier advances), Cr input tax, and the net out of the stock still on hand from what the invoice billed, else cost of sales (freight for charges). The same supplier credit note number twice is refused (409 `DUPLICATE_SUPPLIER_CREDIT_NOTE`).

**Who can call it:** People and API clients · **Scope:** `supplier_invoice:approve` · **Idempotency-Key:** required
