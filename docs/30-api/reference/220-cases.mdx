### Customer: open a case with a supplier

<span className="nh-method nh-method--post">POST</span> `/v1/cases`

A payment, delivery or goods case names the order (`orderId`); an account or other case names the supplier (`sellerOrganizationId`) if it names no order. A goods case also names the lines and what is wrong (`lines`, `reason`), and raises a dispute on them: only their value is held, and the case ends when the dispute does. A payment, delivery or other case on an escrow order holds the whole escrow until it ends. A payment or delivery case the facts already settle (paid in full, delivered) comes back resolved.

**Who can call it:** People and API clients · **Scope:** `case:open`
