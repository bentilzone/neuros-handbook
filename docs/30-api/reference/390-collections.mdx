### Collect through the partner bank, and set the dispute window

<span className="nh-method nh-method--put">PUT</span> `/v1/collections/settings`

With collection on, each customer gets a virtual account at the partner bank: what they pay into it is matched to their orders. `releaseCondition` is how an escrow order paid into one releases: after the dispute window following delivery (`disputeWindowHours`, 1 to 168), or when the buyer confirms.
