### Seller: credit part of an invoice

<span className="nh-method nh-method--post">POST</span> `/v1/credit-notes`

The amount includes tax, split in the invoice’s proportion. Reduces what the customer owes and reverses that revenue and tax. On a paid invoice, what goes beyond the outstanding comes off its latest payments and is held for the customer as an advance. At most the invoice total less what is already credited.
