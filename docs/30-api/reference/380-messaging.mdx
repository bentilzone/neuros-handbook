### Your provider or agent platform posts a receipt or a reply

<span className="nh-method nh-method--post">POST</span> `/v1/inbound/messaging/{inboxKey}`

Signed like a Neuros webhook (`Neuros-Signature`, with your messaging secret), within 5 minutes. Stored once by `id` and answered 202; a repeat changes nothing. `message.status` names the message (`messageId` or `providerRef`) and its `status`; `message.inbound` gives `channel`, `from` and `text` (STOP and START change the reseller’s consent).
