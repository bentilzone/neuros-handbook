One list of real products, shared by every company, reviewed by the platform.

## Finding products
**Catalogue** shows the category tree, a search across names, brands, part numbers and barcodes, and each product's detail:
- attributes;
- units and conversions;
- tracking;
- the category it sits in.

Everyone can read it.

## Contributing a product (sellers)
When a product you sell isn't in the catalogue:
1. **Contribute** it: choose its category, then fill in the attributes that category asks for. The form is built from the category, so only its own attributes are accepted. Add its units and conversions.
2. Your contribution is a **draft** only you see. Edit it until it's right.
3. **Submit for review.** A product with a barcode already in the catalogue is refused, and you are pointed to the existing one. A matching brand and part number is flagged to the reviewer.
4. The platform **publishes** it (now everyone can offer it) or **sends it back** with a note, in which case you correct it and submit again.

**My contributions** lists yours and where each stands.

## Proposing a category
If no category fits, propose one under an existing branch. You can rename your proposal, add sub-categories under it or withdraw it while it waits. It becomes usable when the platform approves it; publishing a product in a proposed category approves the category with it.

## The platform's side
Under **Platform → Catalogue**, reviewers work the queue (publish, ask for changes, retire), maintain categories and their attributes, and switch product types on or off.
