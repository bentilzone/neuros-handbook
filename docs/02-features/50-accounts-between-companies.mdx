Before a buyer sees a seller's private prices, they hold an [account](../01-glossary/40-relationships.mdx#account-trading-relationship) with that seller.

## The buyer asks
Under **Suppliers → Find sellers**, the buyer finds the seller and asks for an account with a message (what they buy, roughly how much, where they deliver). The marketplace offers the same button on a seller's offer. The account shows as **Waiting for approval** on both sides.

## Or the seller invites
Under **Customers**, the seller can invite a buyer they already trade with.

## The seller answers
Under **Customers**, the seller opens the request and either:
- **Approves it with terms:**
  - the price list the buyer gets;
  - the payment modes allowed and the default, with payment days;
  - credit allowed, and up to how much;
  - the largest single order;
  - the dates;
  - private notes.
- **Declines** it.

The buyer sees the decision immediately: an approved buyer now finds the seller's customer-only offers with their own prices.

## Over time
- **Change terms** at any time; the buyer's prices follow at once.
- **Suspend** with a reason when trading must pause (say, overdue invoices), and **reinstate** later.
- **End** closes the account for good. Either side may end it.

## The buyer's side
Under **Suppliers**, the buyer sees each account, its status and the terms that concern them: price list name, payment modes, credit. They can record their own **supplier code** for the seller. Only they see it.
