How a seller puts a product on sale, decides who sees it, and how Neuros works out each buyer's price.

## 1. Create an offer
Under **Offers → New offer**, pick the catalogue product and set your terms:
- your SKU, standard price and unit;
- quantity breaks;
- [visibility](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#visibility);
- [supply](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#supply-stock-mode) and lead time;
- warranty, minimum order and order steps;
- whether buyers see your quantity;
- where you deliver.

Prices start in your company's currency. The offer is a **draft** until you publish it.

## 2. Publish
Publishing puts it in the marketplace for the buyers its visibility allows, within seconds. Published offers count towards your plan's limit. **Withdraw** takes it off sale and keeps its history.

## 3. Give customers their prices
Under **Pricing**:
- **Price lists:** create one (name, currency, dates), add offers with their own quantity breaks, then attach it to customers' accounts in their terms.
- **Contract prices:** a negotiated price for one customer on one offer, with a minimum quantity, your reference and dates. End it when it no longer applies.
- **Promotions:** percent off, amount off or a fixed price, on chosen offers, for every buyer or approved customers only, between dates. Pause, resume or end it.

## 4. How the price is decided
Neuros uses one rule everywhere, the [pricing precedence](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#pricing-precedence):
1. the customer's contract price;
2. else their price list, at the highest quantity break reached;
3. else your standard price or the offer's own break.

The best promotion then applies to 2 or 3, never to a contract. Prices convert between units through the product's conversions and are rounded to the currency's precision.

The **price check** on Offers and Pricing shows exactly what a chosen customer would pay for a quantity, step by step. It is the same calculation buyers get.

## What the buyer sees
- A public offer shows your standard price to everyone.
- Your approved customers see *their* price, with its source (*Your price list*, *Your contract price*).
- Nobody sees your other customers' prices, your lists' contents or your costs.

## Live
Offers, price lists, contracts and promotions update on every colleague's screen as they change, and in the marketplace for buyers.
