Every company keeps its books in Neuros, whatever it trades. Neuros posts what it can itself; people post the rest, with approval.

## The chart of accounts
Under **Ledger → Accounts**, your accounts are grouped by type with the roles each plays. Click one to see its recent entries.
- **Add** an account: code, name, type and reporting group, optionally a foreign currency for a foreign bank account.
- **Edit** names and groups.
- **Archive** an account that is no longer used. An account with a balance or a role can't be archived, and an account's type is fixed once it has postings.

## Automatic postings
When stock is received, adjusted, counted or written off, Neuros posts the journal itself, in the same step:

| Event | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Receipt | Inventory | Goods received not invoiced |
| Stock gain | Inventory | Stock gains |
| Stock loss | Stock losses | Inventory |

**Posting rules** decide which of your accounts plays each role. Invoices, payments and settlements will post the same way as those phases arrive.

## Manual journals
For everything else (capital, rent, salaries, corrections):
1. **New journal:** a date, a memo and at least two lines, each an account with a debit or a credit. It can't be saved until debits equal credits. Receivables, payables, inventory and other [control accounts](../01-glossary/70-ledger.mdx#control-account) aren't offered.
2. **Submit** it. It waits for approval.
3. A colleague with posting rights **approves and posts** it, or **sends it back** to draft with a reason. With segregation of duties on, the person who prepared it can't post it.
4. A draft can be edited or **cancelled**. A posted journal is never changed: **reverse** it, with a reason, and Neuros posts the mirror image.

Posting and reversing need a session signed in with two-step verification.

## Periods
Under **Periods**, each month in your timezone is:
- **Open:** posting allowed.
- **Closed:** posting only with a recorded reason. Earlier months first; no drafts left in it.
- **Locked:** never again.

A closed month can be reopened with a reason.

## Reports
- **Trial balance:** every account's balance on any date, debits beside credits, and whether they balance.
- **FX revaluation:** restates foreign-currency accounts at the rate on a date. The difference posts as an unrealised gain or loss.

Exchange rates are entered by one person and approved by another; Neuros never guesses a missing one.

## Live
Journals, accounts, periods and rules update for everyone in the ledger as colleagues act.
