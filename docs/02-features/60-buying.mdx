How a buyer finds what they need across sellers and sees what they would pay.

## Search and filter
**Marketplace** shows offers you may see:
- every public offer;
- customer-only offers from sellers who approved your account;
- invite-only offers you were invited to.

Search by product, brand, part number or seller. Sort by best match, newest or name. Narrow with the filters: availability, category, brand and seller, each with the count it would leave.

## Read an offer
Each card shows the product, the seller, the price (the standard price on public offers, or *Price for you*), and its [availability](../01-glossary/50-marketplace.mdx#availability). Open it for:
- the product's details and the seller's SKU;
- minimum order and order steps;
- supply and lead time, and where they deliver.

## Your price
On an offer from a seller you have an account with, enter a quantity and a unit to see **your price**: unit price, line total and where it came from (*Your contract price*, *Your price list*, *Standard price*), with any promotion. It is computed by the seller's own rules, so it is what you'd be charged.

## No account yet?
Ask for one from the offer. The seller sees your request under Customers. When they approve, their customer-only offers and your prices appear, live.

## Ask for a quote
For a larger order, or delivery to price, choose **Request a quote** on the offer, or **Request a quote** under Quotes for several of one supplier's offers. The supplier answers with a quotation: fixed prices and terms until a date. Accept it or decline it with a reason; they may send a revision. See Quotes in the [reseller guide](../12-reseller/75-buying-quotes.mdx).

## Order
Draft an **order** to one supplier at your price, or place one from an accepted quotation, then submit it. **Add to order** on a marketplace offer builds the draft as you browse: one draft per supplier, which you can edit until you submit it. Above your purchase limit, someone else approves it first. The supplier accepts it, and you follow its payment and stock status beside it. See Orders in the [reseller guide](../12-reseller/77-buying-orders.mdx). The whole flow on both sides, with a video: [Quote to cash](./65-quote-to-cash.mdx).

## Pay and receive
Pay by the arrangement on your account: tell the supplier you paid a transfer (with the slip), pay into escrow and release it when satisfied, or pay invoices on credit by their due date. You are emailed when the supplier accepts or rejects your order, dispatches it (with the tracking reference) and confirms your payment; your billing contact gets each invoice. Confirm receipt when it arrives. See Orders and Invoices in the [reseller guide](../12-reseller/77-buying-orders.mdx).
