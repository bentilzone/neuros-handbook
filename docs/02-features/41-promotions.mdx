A [promotion](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#promotion) is how you run the offers your sales team used to work out by hand. It can be:
- 5% off once an order reaches ₦1,000,000;
- a free fitting for every 24 breakers;
- 4% off cable and breakers bought together;
- something for your Gold customers only, or for customers who have not ordered in two months.

Neuros applies it on every order, through the app and your AI agent alike. Each promotion is written out in a sentence, so customers can read it, and it never gives more than the budget you set.

## Kinds

| Kind | What it does | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Percent off, amount off, fixed price | Changes the unit price of the offers it covers | In each line's unit price |
| [Volume discount](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#volume-discount) | A bigger percent off the more of an offer is on one line: 5% from 10, 8% from 50 | In the line's unit price |
| [Order discount](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#order-discount) | A percent or an amount off the whole order once it reaches a value | Spread over the lines, before tax, as each line's discount |
| [Buy X, get Y free](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#free-goods) | Free goods for every so many bought, up to a number per order | A free line on the order, at zero |
| [Bundle](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#bundle) | A percent off offers bought together, at least so many of each | Spread over those lines, as their discount |

The first three kinds change one line, as promotions always have. The last three look at the whole order. None of them touch a contract price or a quoted price: those are agreed already.

When several apply, Neuros takes them in this order:
1. The best line promotion, plus any marked **Stacks with others**.
2. Bundles.
3. The best order discount, plus any that stack.
4. Free goods.

## Who gets it

Besides **every buyer** or **approved customers only**, a promotion can name conditions. A customer must meet all of them:
- your **tier** for the customer (for example Gold), or your **delivery zone**, as set on the customer's profile;
- **named customers**;
- **first order only**: a customer who has never placed an order with you;
- **quiet for at least** some days: no order placed in that time.

## The budget

Set any of these caps, or none:

| Cap | Counts |
|---|---|
| Orders in all | Orders placed with the promotion |
| Per customer | Orders one customer placed with it |
| Per day | Orders placed with it on one of your days |
| Discount given, at most | What it took off, with free goods at the price the customer would have paid |

The caps are firm:
- An order counts when it is **placed**. The count is kept in the promotion's [redemption](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#redemption) list.
- If the cap is reached between pricing a cart and placing it, the order is priced again without the promotion and comes back for the customer to check. This applies even to an AI agent's 30-minute quote.
- A rejected or cancelled order gives its share back.
- When a cap is reached, the promotion stops applying, and the people who manage your pricing are told once.

## Customers read it in words

- Every promotion carries its terms as a sentence: "Buy 24 of Breaker 32A, get 1 of Pipe fitting free".
- An order lists **Offers applied**, each with what it saved.
- A draft that is close to an order discount says how much more it needs: "Add ₦443,200 more for: 3% off your order when it reaches ₦1,000,000".
- Your AI agent reads the same out when it quotes a cart.

## Tell resellers

**Tell resellers**, in a promotion's panel, sends the offer to your resellers' chat as a marketing message. It reaches only resellers who:
- qualify for the promotion;
- order through your AI agent;
- agreed to marketing.

Each reseller gets it once per promotion, and none gets more than one marketing message from you a week. Quiet hours and consent apply as for every [message to resellers](./46-messages-to-resellers.mdx).
