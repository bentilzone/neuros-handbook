How a business becomes a company on Neuros, from first sign-up to its first day of trading.

## 1. Create your sign-in
Register with your name, email and a password, then confirm the email from the link Neuros sends. This is *your* sign-in, not your company's: the same login later works for every company you belong to.

## 2. Apply for your company
Signed in with no company yet, you apply for one:
- the legal and trading name;
- the country, which sets the company's functional currency and timezone;
- the registration number, address and contacts;
- what you want to do: supply, distribute, purchase, or a combination.

Then upload the documents asked for, such as the certificate of incorporation, and **submit**. You can save and come back to a draft.

## 3. Review by the platform
A reviewer at Neuros checks the application and the documents.
- **Under review:** they are looking at it.
- **Resubmission requested:** something must be corrected. The reasons are listed; fix them and submit again.
- **Approved:** the company is created with the [capabilities](../01-glossary/10-companies-and-people.mdx#capability) the reviewer granted. These may differ from what you asked for: capabilities are verified facts about your business, not options.
- **Rejected:** with the reason.

Whoever applied becomes the company's [system administrator](../01-glossary/10-companies-and-people.mdx#system-administrator).

## 4. Choose a plan
A new company must choose a [plan](../01-glossary/80-plans-and-billing.mdx#plan-package) before anything else opens. Anyone with billing rights picks one and gives the billing contact, address and tax ID invoices need. The first time, a plan can start with a trial.

## 5. Set up and trade
With a plan, the company's menu opens according to its capabilities:
- **Everyone:** Dashboard, Catalogue, Ledger, and the system pages (Members, Roles, API clients, Audit log, Settings).
- **Sellers** (supply or distribution): Offers, Pricing, Customers, Inventory.
- **Buyers** (purchasing or distribution): Marketplace, Suppliers.
