Each feature followed across everyone it touches: the seller, the buyer, your team and your books. Read these once; the role guides then show the same features page by page.

1. [Joining Neuros](./10-joining-neuros.mdx): sign-up, application, review, plan.
2. [People and access](./20-people-and-access.mdx): members, roles, two-step verification, segregation of duties, API clients, audit.
3. [The shared catalogue](./30-catalogue.mdx): finding, contributing and reviewing products; proposing categories.
4. [Selling](./40-selling.mdx): offers, price lists, contracts, promotions and how a price is decided.
5. [Promotions](./41-promotions.mdx): order discounts, free goods, bundles and volume discounts; who gets them; firm budgets; offers in words and to resellers’ chat.
6. [Selling through AI agents](./45-selling-through-agents.mdx): agents on WhatsApp, SMS and email, resellers invited by phone, orders by chat.
7. [Messages to resellers](./46-messages-to-resellers.mdx): invitation codes and order news in the reseller’s chat, directly or through your agent platform.
8. [Collections and escrow](./47-collections-and-escrow.mdx): a virtual account per customer, payments matched to orders, escrow released after the dispute window, less the platform fee.
9. [Delivery runs and the delivery code](./48-delivery-code.mdx): each driver’s day, a code only the customer has, and the delivery recorded at the door.
10. [Service cases](./49-service-cases.mdx): anything a customer raises, in the app or by chat; escrow held while open; your times; resolved from the records.
11. [Accounts between companies](./50-accounts-between-companies.mdx): requests, terms, suspension and ending.
12. [Buying](./60-buying.mdx): the marketplace and your price.
13. [Quote to cash](./65-quote-to-cash.mdx): one order from the price to the money, on both sides and in the books.
14. [Stock](./70-stock.mdx): receipts, conditions, adjustments, transfers, counts, reorder, valuation.
15. [Your books](./80-books.mdx): accounts, automatic and manual journals, periods, reports.
16. [Integrations, dashboards and reports](./85-integrations-and-reports.mdx): webhooks, imports, the dashboard, reports and the year-end close.
17. [Plans and billing](./90-plans-and-billing.mdx): plans, limits, invoices, late payment.
18. [Support access](./95-support-access.mdx): how Neuros staff look in, and only with a grant.
