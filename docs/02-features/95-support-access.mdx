Neuros staff can't browse a company's business. When support needs to look, it happens through a **support grant**: time-limited, read-only, for a stated purpose, approved by someone else and recorded on both sides.

## How a grant works
1. A support person **requests** access to one company, giving the purpose, how long they need and, optionally, the support ticket it answers.
2. Someone else on the platform team **approves** or **rejects** it. Nobody approves their own request.
3. While **active**, the support person can open the company read-only. A banner on every page shows they are acting under the grant and when it ends.
4. It ends when it **expires** or is **revoked**. Everything viewed under it is in the company's audit log and the platform's.

## What the company sees
The company's audit log shows each grant: who asked, who approved, the purpose, the period, and what was looked at. The company never needs to share a password.
