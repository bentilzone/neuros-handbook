Neuros staff can't browse a company's business. When support needs to look, they ask, and **your company decides**. Access is time-limited, read-only, for a stated purpose, and recorded in your audit log. Nobody at Neuros can approve it for you.

## How a grant works
1. A support person **requests** access to your company, giving the purpose, how long they need and, optionally, the support ticket it answers.
2. Your system administrator is emailed. Anyone in your company with the right to approve support (`support:approve`) opens **Settings → Support access** and **grants** or **declines** it.
3. Once granted, the clock starts. Support can open your company **read-only** for the time asked. A banner on every page they see shows they are acting under your grant and when it ends.
4. It ends when the time runs out, or sooner if you **end access now**. Support can also cancel or end it themselves.

## What you see
**Settings → Support access** lists every request: who asked, the purpose, the duration, its state and, once used, what was looked at. Every page support opens under a grant is in your audit log.

A company without a plan can still grant support access, so a locked company can always get help.
