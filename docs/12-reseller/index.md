You buy from the sellers you have accounts with. You find their offers in the Marketplace, ask for accounts on Suppliers, and see the price agreed for you. You don't sell or hold stock in Neuros, so Offers, Pricing, Customers and Inventory aren't in your menu.

This guide walks the app as **Ikeja Hardware Resellers**, a demo reseller in Nigeria whose books are in naira. It follows the sidebar from top to bottom:

| Section | Pages |
|---|---|
| Main | [Dashboard](./10-dashboard.mdx), [Catalogue](./20-catalogue.mdx) |
| Buy | [Marketplace](./60-marketplace.mdx), [Suppliers](./70-suppliers.mdx) |
| Finance | [Ledger](./90-ledger.mdx) |
| System | [Members](./100-members.mdx), [Roles](./110-roles.mdx), [API clients](./120-api-clients.mdx), [Audit log](./130-audit-log.mdx) |
| You | [Settings](./140-settings.mdx), every tab |

Ikeja Hardware buys from two distributors. One gives it the Gold resellers price list and a contract price on a submersible pump. The other has a contract price on a solar pump. The marketplace shows each price as Ikeja would pay it.

:::tip[Your currency]
Your books and the amounts you enter are in your company's functional currency, here NGN. A seller abroad prices in their own currency, and you see that price as it is.
:::
