**Messages** are what Neuros sends to your resellers’ chat: the code from an invitation by phone, and news of their orders, from received to delivered. They go from your own WhatsApp number or SMS sender, either through Neuros’s messaging provider or through your agent platform, which sends them in the conversation it already holds. See [Messages to resellers](../02-features/46-messages-to-resellers.mdx) for the whole flow.

Messages go only to customers your AI agent knows, that is, customers with the numbers they write from (see <PageLink who={props.who} page="agents">AI agents</PageLink>). While messaging is **off**, nothing is sent: you pass invitation codes on yourself.

## Sent

Every message, newest first: who it went to and on which number, which message it was, and how it fared.

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| Waiting | Queued. Neuros sends it within seconds, or after quiet hours, or after a failed try |
| Sent | Your provider or agent platform took it |
| Delivered | It reached the reseller’s phone |
| Read | The reseller opened it (WhatsApp only, when they share read receipts) |
| Failed | Five tries failed. The reason is shown; **Try again** starts again |
| Not sent | Neuros did not send it, and says why: the reseller opted out, messaging was switched off, or you have no active number on that channel |

Open a message to see its full text, the numbers it went from and to, when it was sent, delivered and read, and each try. A message with an invitation code never shows the code here: it shows dots.

## Texts

Each message has Neuros’s text. You can write your own for any of them.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/messages/templates`} />

| Message | Sent when | Category |
|---|---|---|
| Invitation code | You invite a reseller by phone, or give them a new code | Code |
| Order received | They place an order, by chat or otherwise | Update |
| Order accepted | You accept it | Update |
| Order rejected | You reject it, with your reason | Update |
| Payment received | You confirm their payment | Update |
| On its way | The delivery is dispatched | Update |
| Delivered | The delivery is recorded as delivered | Update |
| Invoice issued | You invoice them | Update |

The category is the one WhatsApp prices messages by.

When messaging is on, the **Connection** card shows **your inbox**, the address your provider or agent platform posts delivery receipts and replies to, and the end of your messaging secret. **Rotate secret** needs two-step verification; the old secret stops at once, so change it in your agent platform at the same time.

## Inbox

Every receipt and reply that reached your inbox, and what Neuros did with it. Each is kept once, however often it is sent. A receipt moves a message forward (sent, delivered, read), never back. A reseller who writes **STOP** gets no more messages; **START** turns them back on. Anything else they write is for your agent to answer, and is left alone here.

## A reseller’s consent

On <PageLink who={props.who} page="customers">Customers</PageLink>, a customer your agent knows has two switches under **Your agent**:

- **Messages to their chat**: off stops every message to them, the invitation code included. It also shows when the reseller turned it off or on themselves.
- **Marketing**: whether they agreed to marketing messages. Neuros sends none yet; campaigns will need it.
