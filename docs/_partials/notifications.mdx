Notifications tell you what happened that concerns you: an order arrived, a payment was confirmed, a change waits for your answer. They are yours alone. Each person sees only their own, in the company they are working in. See [notification](../01-glossary/90-platform.mdx#notification).

## The bell

The bell at the top right counts your unread notifications. It turns orange when some of them need you to act. Click it to see the latest beside the page. Click one to open what it is about; that also marks it read.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/notifications/bell`} />

- New notifications arrive as they happen; you do not need to reload.
- One that needs you to act, such as a new order to accept, also shows a short note at the top of the screen.
- Reading a notification in one tab marks it read in your other tabs too.
- The number in your browser tab's title is your unread count.

## All your notifications

**See all** opens this page. It lists your unread notifications first; choose **All** to see the ones you have read. Filter by category, and use **Mark all read** to clear them.

## Who is told what

You are told about things you can act on, or that you started. You are never told about something you did yourself.

| When | Who is told |
|---|---|
| A customer asks for a quotation | The seller's people who prepare quotations |
| A quotation is sent | The buyer who asked for it, and people who can accept quotations |
| A quotation is accepted or declined | The seller's people who prepared it |
| An order is placed | The seller's people who can accept orders (also by email) |
| An order is accepted or rejected | The buyer's people who placed it (also by email) |
| A change to an order is proposed, approved or rejected | The other side: who placed or accepted the order, and people who can change orders |
| An order is cancelled | The other side |
| An order ships | The buyer's people who placed it (also by email, with the tracking reference) |
| A delivery is recorded | The buyer, to confirm receipt |
| A delivery fails | Both sides' people who handle shipments |
| A buyer confirms receipt | The seller's people who handle shipments |
| An invoice is issued | The buyer's people who can see bills (the billing contact is emailed the invoice) |
| A buyer says they paid | The seller's people who record payments |
| A payment is confirmed or not received | The buyer's people who placed the order (confirmed: also by email) |
| An order is funded, released or refunded in escrow | The seller's people who ship (funded) or see payments (released); the buyer (refunded) |
| A customer disputes part of an order | The seller's people who answer disputes (also by email) |
| Someone writes on a dispute | The other side: who raised or answered it, and people who answer disputes |
| A dispute is escalated | Both sides |
| A dispute is decided | The buyer's people who raised it (also by email) |
| A dispute is withdrawn | The seller's people who answer disputes |
| A customer asks to return goods | The seller's people who handle returns (also by email) |
| A return is authorised, rejected or closed | The buyer's people who asked for it (authorised and closed: also by email) |
| An order is over the purchase limit of whoever submitted it | The buyer's people who can approve orders |
| A requisition is over the purchase limit of whoever submitted it | Your people who can approve requisitions |
| A requisition is approved | Your people who manage purchase orders, and whoever raised it |
| A requisition is rejected | Whoever raised it |
| A receipt has a product that came instead of the one ordered | Your people who can approve purchase orders |
| A supplier invoice does not match the order or what was received | Your people who can approve supplier invoices |
| A journal is submitted for posting | Your people who can post journals |
| A journal is posted or sent back | Whoever prepared it |
| A stock adjustment is proposed, or a count submitted | Your people who can approve adjustments or counts |
| An adjustment or count is decided | Whoever proposed or submitted it |
| A company asks you for an account | The seller's people who manage accounts |
| Your account request is approved, declined, suspended or ended | The buyer's people who ask for accounts, and whoever asked |
| Neuros support asks for access | Your people who can grant support access |
| A Neuros invoice is issued, due or paid | Your people who manage the company's plan |
| A catalogue proposal is published or not | Whoever proposed it, and people who contribute products |

When nobody in a company fits, its system administrator is told, so nothing that needs an answer is missed. A posting the system made itself, such as stock valuation, tells nobody.

You choose which categories reach your bell and your email, and whether you get a morning summary, under <PageLink who={props.who} page="settings" hash="notifications">Settings → Notifications</PageLink>.

## Common questions

**Why did a colleague get a notification I did not?** Notifications follow what each person can do. Someone who can accept orders hears of new orders; someone who only handles stock does not.

**How long are notifications kept?** Read ones for 90 days; everything for a year.
