Disputes and returns is where you answer customers who say part of a delivery is wrong, and handle goods they want to send back. Only the part in question is held: the rest of the order is paid and closed as usual. See [dispute](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#dispute) and [return](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#return-rma).

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Waiting for the seller | Yours to answer, by the date shown |
| Waiting for the buyer | You asked the customer something |
| Escalated | Not answered in time, or either side asked: an administrator decides |
| Decided | You decided it; the outcome is on the dispute |
| Withdrawn | The customer withdrew it |

## Answer a dispute
{props.who === 'distributor' && <Shot id="distributor/disputes/detail" />}

A dispute shows the lines and quantities in question, what they are worth at the order's price with tax (**Held**), the customer's reason and description, their photos and documents, and the messages between you. Open a piece of evidence to see it; add your own with **Add a photo or document**.

Answer within **3 working days** of it being raised or of the customer's last answer, in your company's timezone. Unanswered, it is escalated to your administrators, and both sides are told. Writing to the customer puts it to *Waiting for the buyer* until they answer.

## Returns

The **Returns** tab lists what customers asked to send back, and returns opened by a dispute you decided as *Take it back*.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Asked | Waiting for you to authorise or reject |
| Authorised | The customer may send the goods, quoting its RMA number |
| Received | In quarantine: not free to sell |

| Closed | Credited |
| Rejected | Refused, with your reason |

## The dispute window
How long after delivery a customer may dispute part of it is set on their account: **Days to dispute a delivery** under <PageLink who={props.who} page="customers">Customers</PageLink>, 7 unless you change it.

## Live
The list and an open dispute or return update as the customer writes, adds evidence or withdraws, and as your team acts.

## Common questions

**Does a dispute stop the whole order?** No. Only the disputed quantities and their value are held. The rest is invoiced, paid and delivered as usual, and on escrow the customer can release everything else.

**Can I undo a decision?** No, a decision is final. A replacement shipment can still be cancelled before it is dispatched; its stock is freed.

**Who decides an escalated dispute?** Your administrators, or anyone with the right to respond to disputes. Neuros does not decide for you.
