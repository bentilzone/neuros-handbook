Quotes is where you price a deal for one customer before they order: the **requests** customers send you, and the **quotations** you draft, send and revise. A quotation is a fixed offer to one customer. Its prices are worked out the way the price check does, and you can set your own price on any line. See [quotation](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#quotation).

<Shot id={`${props.who}/quotes/list`} />

## Statuses

| Status | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | You are preparing it. The customer cannot see it. | Edit, Send |
| Sent | The customer has it and can accept it until its validity ends | Revise |
| Accepted | The customer agreed the prices and terms | Nothing more: the customer places the order |
| Declined | The customer said no, with a reason you see | Revise |
| Expired | Its validity ran out before the customer decided | Revise |
| Replaced by a revision | You sent a newer revision, which replaced this one | Open the newer one |

## Requests received

**Requests received** lists what customers asked you to quote: the products and quantities, when they need them and where to deliver. A customer asks from your offer in their marketplace, or from their own Quotes page. They need an approved [account](../01-glossary/40-relationships.mdx) with you, and can only ask about offers they can see.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/quotes/requests`} />

Open a request and choose **Prepare a quotation**. The draft starts from the request's lines; change anything before you send it.

| Request status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Waiting for a quote | Nothing sent yet |
| Quoted | You sent at least one quotation |
| Accepted | The customer accepted one of your quotations |
| Cancelled | The customer withdrew it. Quotations still open on it are declined. |

## Prepare a quotation

**New quotation** starts a draft for any approved customer, without a request. Saving prices every line for that customer: their contract, their price list, quantity breaks and promotions, exactly as the [price check](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#price-check) does. Tax comes from each offer's tax code, at the rate in your country.

## Send, revise, and what the customer does

{props.who === 'distributor' && <Shot id="distributor/quotes/draft" />}

- **Send** gives the customer the quotation and fixes its prices: they no longer change, even if your price list does. It also sets the validity date if you left it blank. A request it answers becomes *Quoted*.
- **Revise** starts the next revision as a draft. It keeps the number, for example *Q-2026-000004 rev 2*. Sending it replaces the revision before it, which can no longer be accepted.
- **The customer** accepts or declines the latest revision you sent, until it expires. Accepting closes their request. Declining tells you their reason, and you can revise.
- **The PDF** icon opens the quotation as the customer receives it.

:::note[Then the order]
The customer then places the order from the accepted quotation, at its prices. It arrives under <PageLink who={props.who} page="orders">Orders</PageLink>. One quotation makes one order.
:::

Seeing quotes needs `quote:read`; preparing, sending and revising them needs `quote:prepare`.

## Live

A new request, or a customer accepting or declining, appears on its own. What you send appears on the customer's screen at the same moment.

## Common questions

**Can the customer see my draft?** No. Until you send it, they cannot find it at all.

**I changed my price list after sending. Does the quotation change?** No. A sent quotation keeps the prices it was sent with. Revise it to send new prices.

**Can I quote a customer without an account?** No. Approve their account first under <PageLink who={props.who} page="customers">Customers</PageLink>.
