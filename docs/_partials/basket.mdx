The basket holds what you are about to buy from several suppliers at once: each open draft order, one per supplier, built with **Add to order** in the <PageLink who={props.who} page="marketplace">Marketplace</PageLink> or **New order** under <PageLink who={props.who} page="buying-orders">Orders</PageLink>. **Check out** sends them together. Each then becomes its own order with its supplier: accepted, paid, delivered and invoiced on its own, and never netted against another. See [basket](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#basket-and-split-checkout).

The basket button in the header shows how many lines are waiting, and opens this page.

## What is in it
One card per supplier: its lines at your price, the total, and the way you pay. **Edit** opens the draft under Orders, to change quantities or remove a line; every save prices it again. A supplier whose account with you no longer allows ordering is shown, and left out of checkout.

## Check out
Tick the orders to send, choose how you pay each one, and optionally where to deliver them all, then **Check out**.

Each order is submitted on its own, exactly as **Submit** on the draft does: prices are checked again, your purchase limit applies, and each supplier accepts or rejects its own. One supplier refusing does not stop the others.

## If one is not sent
The checkout lists each order and what happened: **Sent to the seller**, **Waiting for your approval** (over your purchase limit), **Accepted** (the supplier accepts automatically), or **Not sent** with the reason, such as prices that changed since you drafted or a payment way the supplier no longer takes. What was not sent stays in your basket: fix it and check it out again.

## Checkouts
The **Checkouts** tab lists every checkout with each of its orders and where each stands now: accepted, paid, reserved, delivered. Each order opens under Orders. Suppliers never see your checkout, or who else you bought from.

## Live
The basket, its count in the header and the Checkouts tab update as your orders change, from your team or the suppliers.

## Common questions

**Can I pay several suppliers in one payment?** No. Each order is paid to its own supplier, by its own arrangement.

**I checked out twice by accident.** A retried checkout sends nothing twice: the same orders, the same answer.

**Why is a supplier missing from the basket?** You have no open draft with them. Use **Add to order** on one of their offers.
