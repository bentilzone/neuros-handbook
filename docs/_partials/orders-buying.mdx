Orders is where you buy: draft an order with one supplier, submit it, and follow it to acceptance. An order is priced at **your** price with that supplier (their contract with you, your price list, quantity breaks and promotions), or at the prices of a quotation you accepted. See [order](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#order).

## Place an order

**New order** drafts an order to one supplier you have an approved account with. To order at a quotation's prices, open the accepted quotation under <PageLink who={props.who} page="buying-quotes">Quotes</PageLink> and choose **Place order**.

You can also build a draft from the <PageLink who={props.who} page="marketplace">Marketplace</PageLink>: **Add to order** on an offer puts it in your open draft with that seller, or starts one.

### Edit a draft

A draft is your basket with one supplier; drafts with several suppliers can be sent together from the <PageLink who={props.who} page="basket">Basket</PageLink>. **Edit** on the draft changes it before you submit: quantities, removing a line, when and where to deliver, your reference and the note. Saving prices every line again at your price now. A draft placed from a quotation keeps the quotation's lines and prices; only the delivery details, reference and note change. To drop the last line, cancel the order.

{props.who === 'reseller' && <Shot id="reseller/buying-orders/edit" />}

The draft is yours alone until you **Submit** it. Submitting checks again that your account is open, that the offers are still on sale, and their prices. If a price changed, the draft shows the new price and asks you to submit again, so you never buy at a price you did not see.

## Approval above your limit

If the order is over your **purchase limit** (set under <PageLink who={props.who} page="members">Members</PageLink>), it waits as *Waiting for approval*. Someone else with approval rights, within their own approval limit, approves it. Then it goes to the supplier.

## After you submit

{props.who === 'reseller' && <Shot id="reseller/buying-orders/detail" />}

- **Waiting for the seller:** the supplier accepts or rejects it. Some suppliers accept automatically.
- **Accepted:** the prices are fixed, and the **Payment** and **Stock** statuses beside it show what happens next.
- **Rejected:** the supplier's reason is shown.

Either of you can **propose a change** before it ships: new quantities (0 removes a line) or a new date. The other side approves or rejects it. **Cancel order** works until it ships; on an accepted order the supplier sees your reason.

## Shipments

Once the supplier ships, the order lists its **shipments**. Open one for what was sent, how it travels and its tracking reference, and the delivery note PDF.

{props.who === 'reseller' && <Shot id="reseller/buying-orders/shipment" />}

**Confirm receipt** when the goods arrive. The supplier is told; if they had not yet marked it delivered, your confirmation does. A failed delivery shows the supplier's reason until it goes out again.

Ordering needs `order:create`; approving needs `order:approve`; changes need `order:amend`; cancelling needs `order:cancel`.

## Paying

{props.who === 'reseller' && <Shot id="reseller/buying-orders/escrow" />}

How you pay is the order's **payment mode**:

- **Bank transfer or prepay:** pay the supplier by transfer, then **I have paid** with the bank reference and the slip. It counts once the supplier confirms the money arrived; if they say it did not, their reason is shown. A prepay order is only reserved and shipped once paid, and one left unpaid too long is cancelled.
- **Credit:** pay each invoice by its due date. What you pay comes back to your available credit.
- **Escrow:** **Pay into escrow** sends the money to the escrow partner, who holds it. The supplier ships once the partner confirms it. **Release to the seller** when you are satisfied; delivery alone never releases it. A dispute holds it.

## Invoices

The supplier's invoices for the order are listed on it; see <PageLink who={props.who} page="buying-invoices">Invoices</PageLink>. Its **Payment** status follows them.

## Live

The supplier accepting, rejecting, proposing a change or shipping appears on its own.

## Common questions

**Why did Submit say prices changed?** The supplier changed a price after you drafted. The draft now shows the new total; submit again if it is still right.

**Can I put two suppliers in one order?** No. An order is with one supplier, so it can be accepted, shipped and invoiced by them alone. Add to order keeps one draft per supplier.

**Can I order a quotation twice?** No. One accepted quotation makes one order. For more, ask for a new quotation.
