Orders is where you buy: draft an order with one supplier, submit it, and follow it to acceptance. An order is priced at **your** price with that supplier (their contract with you, your price list, quantity breaks and promotions), or at the prices of a quotation you accepted. See [order](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#order).

## Place an order

**New order** drafts an order to one supplier you have an approved account with. To order at a quotation's prices, open the accepted quotation under <PageLink who={props.who} page="buying-quotes">Quotes</PageLink> and choose **Place order**.

The draft is yours alone until you **Submit** it. Submitting checks again that your account is open, that the offers are still on sale, and their prices. If a price changed, the draft shows the new price and asks you to submit again, so you never buy at a price you did not see.

## Approval above your limit

If the order is over your **purchase limit** (set under <PageLink who={props.who} page="members">Members</PageLink>), it waits as *Waiting for approval*. Someone else with approval rights, within their own approval limit, approves it. Then it goes to the supplier.

## After you submit

{props.who === 'reseller' && <Shot id="reseller/buying-orders/detail" />}

- **Waiting for the seller:** the supplier accepts or rejects it. Some suppliers accept automatically.
- **Accepted:** the prices are fixed, and the **Payment** and **Stock** statuses beside it show what happens next.
- **Rejected:** the supplier's reason is shown.

Either of you can **propose a change** before it ships: new quantities (0 removes a line) or a new date. The other side approves or rejects it. **Cancel order** works until it ships; on an accepted order the supplier sees your reason.

Ordering needs `order:create`; approving needs `order:approve`; changes need `order:amend`; cancelling needs `order:cancel`.

## Live

The supplier accepting, rejecting or proposing a change appears on its own.

## Common questions

**Why did Submit say prices changed?** The supplier changed a price after you drafted. The draft now shows the new total; submit again if it is still right.

**Can I order a quotation twice?** No. One accepted quotation makes one order. For more, ask for a new quotation.
