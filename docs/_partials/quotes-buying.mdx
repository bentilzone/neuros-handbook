Quotes is where you ask suppliers for a price before you order, and decide on what they send. A **request for quotation** asks one supplier to price some of their offers for you. A **quotation** is their answer: fixed prices and terms, valid until a date. See [quotation](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#quotation).

## Request a quote

**Request a quote** asks one supplier you have an approved account with. You can also ask from an offer in the <PageLink who={props.who} page="marketplace">Marketplace</PageLink>: **Request a quote** under your price.

| Request status | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting for a quote | The supplier has not answered yet | Cancel request |
| Quoted | The supplier sent at least one quotation | Accept or decline it; Cancel request |
| Accepted | You accepted one of its quotations | Nothing more: the order follows |
| Cancelled | You withdrew it, with a reason the supplier sees | Nothing more |

Cancelling a request also declines the quotations still open on it.

## Decide on a quotation

Open a quotation from **Quotations** or from its request. It shows each line's price, tax and amount, the freight, the total, and the supplier's lead time, delivery and terms.

{props.who === 'reseller' && <Shot id="reseller/buying-quotes/detail" />}

- **Accept** agrees the prices and terms. You can add a note for the supplier. It closes the request.
- **Decline** needs a reason, which the supplier sees. They may send a revision.
- **The PDF** icon opens the quotation as a document.

You can accept only the **latest** revision the supplier sent, and only until it is **valid until**. An older revision shows *Replaced by a revision*, with a link to the newer one. One past its date shows *Expired*: ask the supplier for a new revision.

:::note[Orders follow]
An accepted quotation becomes an order with the next part of Neuros. Until then, accepting records the agreement.
:::

Seeing quotes needs `quote:read`; requesting, accepting and declining needs `quote:request`.

## Live

A quotation the supplier sends appears on its own, and so does a new revision.

## Common questions

**Why can't I accept this quotation?** It is either past its date, or the supplier sent a newer revision. Open the newest one, or ask them to revise.

**Do I see the supplier's draft while they work on it?** No. You see a quotation only once they send it.
