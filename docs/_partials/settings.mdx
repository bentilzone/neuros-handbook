### Capabilities

<Shot id={`${props.who}/settings/company-capabilities`} />

What kind of company you are: supply, distribution, purchasing. Capabilities decide which pages and actions you have. Neuros grants them when it approves your company, after checking you can trade that way. They are not switches here: ask Neuros support to add or remove one, and the change is recorded in the change log. See [capabilities](../01-glossary/10-companies-and-people.mdx).

### Change log

<Shot id={`${props.who}/settings/company-log`} />

Every change to the company's details: who made it, when, and from what to what.

## Support access

<Shot id={`${props.who}/settings/support`} />

Neuros support can see your company only when you grant a request: read-only, for the time they asked. Each request says why support is asking and for how long. **Grant access** or **Decline** it. **End access now** stops a grant early. Every page support opens is in your <PageLink who={props.who} page="audit-log">audit log</PageLink>. See [support access](../02-features/95-support-access.mdx).
