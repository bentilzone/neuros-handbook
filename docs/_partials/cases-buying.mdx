Cases is where you raise anything with a supplier: a payment that is not showing, a delivery that is late, goods that are not right, a question about your account. See [service case](../01-glossary/49-service-cases.mdx#service-case).

<Shot id={`${props.who}/buying-cases/list`} />

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| With the supplier | Waiting for their answer |
| Waiting on you | The supplier wrote to you |
| Resolved | Ended, with what was done |
| Withdrawn | You withdrew it |

## Follow a case

{props.who === 'reseller' && <Shot id="reseller/buying-cases/detail" />}

Open a case to read the supplier's answers and write back. Your answer puts it back with them. **Withdraw** ends it. A goods case's dispute stays open until you withdraw it under <PageLink who={props.who} page="buying-disputes">Disputes and returns</PageLink>.

If you order by chat, the supplier's AI agent can open and follow cases for you, and the supplier's answers reach your chat.

Opening and writing need `case:open`.
