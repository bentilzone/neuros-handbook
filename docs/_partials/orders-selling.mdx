Orders is where your customers' orders arrive: you accept or reject them, reserve their stock, and agree any changes. An order is one customer buying from you, at prices fixed when they priced it. See [order](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#order).

<Shot id={`${props.who}/orders/list`} />

Each order shows three statuses side by side, because each moves on its own:

| Status | What it says | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Order | Where the agreement stands | Waiting for the seller, Accepted, Rejected, Cancelled, Completed |
| Payment | Whether it is paid | Awaiting payment, Part paid, Paid, Refunded |
| Stock | What is reserved or shipped | Not reserved, Partly reserved, Stock reserved, then dispatch and delivery |

You never see a customer's draft. An order appears here when they submit it, or once their approver approves it.

## Accept or reject

{props.who === 'distributor' && <Shot id="distributor/orders/detail" />}

**Accept** confirms the order. What it does depends on how the customer pays:

- **On credit:** it claims the order's total from the customer's [credit](../01-glossary/40-relationships.mdx#credit-limit). Over their available credit it is refused, unless you choose a **credit override** you approved under <PageLink who={props.who} page="customers">Customers</PageLink>.
- **On credit or by bank transfer:** it reserves the stock now, by your **When stock is short** setting (under Settings, My company).
- **Paying first or by escrow:** stock is reserved once they have paid.

**Reject** declines the order with a reason the customer sees.

Prices do not change after acceptance: a later price list or offer change only affects new orders.

:::tip[Accepting automatically]
Switch on **Accept their orders automatically** in a customer's terms, or set **Order acceptance** to automatic for every customer. An order that fails the credit or stock check still waits for you.
:::

## Stock short

With the default setting, accepting reserves what is free and **backorders** the rest. A line shows how much is reserved and how much is backordered. When stock arrives, **Reserve stock** reserves what the order is still waiting for. Lines for special-order offers are always backordered.

## Change an order

Either side can **propose a change** to an order that is waiting or accepted and not yet shipped: a new quantity per line (0 removes it) or a new delivery date. The other side approves or rejects it.

## Cancel

**Cancel order** on an accepted order gives back its reserved stock and claimed credit, with a reason the customer sees. Once part of it has shipped it can no longer be cancelled.

Accepting and rejecting needs `order:accept`; reserving stock needs `order:allocate`; changes need `order:amend`; cancelling needs `order:cancel`.

## Payment

{props.who === 'distributor' && <Shot id="distributor/orders/payment" />}

An accepted order shows how it is being paid under **Payment**. Nothing is paid on the customer's word alone:

- **Bank transfer or prepay:** the customer says they have paid, with the bank reference and often the slip. Check your bank, then **Confirm received** (it records a receipt that pays the order's invoices, and holds anything over for its next ones) or **Not received** with a reason they see. **Money received** records a payment that came without a notice. See [payment notice](../01-glossary/40-relationships.mdx#payment-notice).
- **Prepay:** the stock is reserved only once the order is fully paid. One still unpaid after your **Cancel unpaid prepay orders after** setting cancels itself and frees its stock, unless the customer has said they paid.
- **Credit:** what the order claimed comes back to their available credit as its invoices are paid.
- **Escrow:** the customer pays into escrow with the partner first; you can reserve and ship only once the partner confirms the funds. They are released to you when the customer releases them, or automatically some days after delivery if they chose that, never on delivery alone. Before release you can **Refund the buyer** with a reason. See [escrow](../01-glossary/40-relationships.mdx#escrow).

## Invoices

An accepted order lists its **invoices** under its shipments. They are issued as it ships, or when you accept it, by your **Invoice orders** setting; see <PageLink who={props.who} page="invoices">Invoices</PageLink>. Its **Payment** status follows them, and an order that is paid and delivered is **Completed**. An invoiced order cannot be cancelled or changed until its invoices are void.

## Live

A new order, an approval, a cancellation or a proposed change appears on its own, on both sides.

## Common questions

**Why was accepting refused?** The order is over the customer's available credit, or your **When stock is short** setting is *Refuse* and stock is short. The message says which.

**The customer changed their price list. Do their orders change?** No. An order keeps the prices it was placed at.
