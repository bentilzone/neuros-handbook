The audit log says who did what, to which record, and why. It covers every change in your company, by a person, an API client or Neuros itself. See [audit trail](../01-glossary/90-platform.mdx).

<Shot id={`${props.who}/audit/log`} />

- **Changes** lists every change to your records. **API requests** lists every call your API clients made.
- **The cards** at the top count events by record type: products, offers, price lists, journals, stock receipts and so on. Click one to filter.
- **Filter** by action, record and time.
- How long each kind is kept depends on your plan, shown at the top right.

## An event

Click an event to see what changed: each field with its old and new value, and the reason when one was given.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/audit/event`} />

Nobody can edit or delete an event, administrators included. When Neuros support views your company under a grant you gave, every page they open is recorded here too.
