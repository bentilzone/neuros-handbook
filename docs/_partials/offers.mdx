An offer is what you sell: your SKU, your price and terms, and who may see it, all on a shared <PageLink who={props.who} page="catalogue">catalogue</PageLink> product. One catalogue product can carry offers from many sellers, and buyers compare them in the <PageLink who={props.who} page="marketplace">marketplace</PageLink>. See [selling and pricing](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx) for the terms.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/offers/list`} />

## Statuses

| Status | What it means | What moves it |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Saved; nobody else sees it | **Publish** (needs `offer:publish`) |
| Published | Live in the marketplace and through the API, for whoever **Who can see it** allows | **Withdraw** |
| Withdrawn | Off sale | Edit and publish again |

Withdrawing an offer does not touch open orders. Editing a published offer changes it for new orders; orders already accepted keep the price and terms they were placed at.

## An offer

Click a row to open it: its price and breaks, fulfilment, where it sells, and a **price check**. Pick a customer and a quantity to see exactly what they would pay and why.

## Live

When a colleague publishes, edits or withdraws an offer, your list updates on its own. So does every buyer's marketplace.

## Common questions

**I published an offer. Why can't a customer see it?** Check **Who can see it**. Approved customers needs an approved account on <PageLink who={props.who} page="customers">Customers</PageLink>, and invite-only needs the buyer picked.

**Can a customer see another customer's price?** No. Each buyer sees only their own price.
