## Post into stock
A saved receipt is a draft. **Post into stock** counts it in: stock rises at the warehouse, valued at the ordered price in your company’s currency at the day’s rate, and the books record it as received but not yet invoiced. The purchase order shows what was received, and is **Received** once every line is in or closed short.

A substitute waits for **Accept substitute** first, and the people who can accept it are notified.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/receiving/detail`} />

**Void** takes a reason and takes the goods back out at exactly the cost they came in at. It is refused once the receipt is invoiced, carries landed cost, or some of what it brought in has already left the warehouse.

## Landed costs
Freight, duty, insurance and handling belong in the cost of what you bought. **New landed cost** on the **Landed costs** tab adds them to one or more posted receipts.

**Allocate and post** spreads the charges and posts them. The panel then shows each line’s share. **Void** takes a reason and is possible only while it is a draft.

## Live
The lists and an open receipt or landed cost update as your team receives, posts and allocates.

## Common questions

**At what price is stock valued?** At the purchase order’s or order’s price. If the supplier’s invoice differs, approving it adjusts the cost of what is still on hand; see <PageLink who={props.who} page="payables">Payables</PageLink>.

**Can I receive more than I ordered?** Only with the exception Over, so it is never by accident.
