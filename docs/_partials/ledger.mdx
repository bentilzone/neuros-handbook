The ledger is your books: every journal, automatic or manual, the accounts they post to, and the balances they add up to. Everything is in your functional currency. See [the ledger](../01-glossary/70-ledger.mdx) and [books, end to end](../02-features/80-books.mdx).

Journals that follow from what you do in Neuros write themselves, through your [posting rules](#posting-rules): for a company that holds stock, every receipt, adjustment and count variance posts its value. You enter manual journals for what Neuros does not record, such as rent, salaries or share capital.

## Journals

<Shot id={`${props.who}/ledger/journals`} />

**From** says where each journal came from: Manual for journals a person entered, or the automatic source, such as Stock.

| Status | What it means | What moves it |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Being prepared; changes nothing | **Submit**, or **Cancel** |
| Waiting for approval | Submitted | **Approve and post**, **Send back** to draft, or **Cancel** |
| Posted | In the books for good | **Reverse** |
| Reversed | Cancelled out by a mirror-image journal; both stay visible | — |
| Cancelled | Closed before posting | — |

A posted journal is never edited or deleted. **Reverse** posts its mirror image and marks the original reversed, so the history shows both.

## Trial balance

<Shot id={`${props.who}/ledger/trial-balance`} />

Every account's balance on the **As of** date, grouped by type, with total debits and credits. **Balanced** confirms they agree.

**Revalue foreign currency** restates each foreign-currency account at the approved rate on that date. The difference posts to unrealised exchange gain or loss. It runs once per account and date, and fails if a closing rate is missing rather than guess. It needs a session signed in with two-step verification.

## Periods

<Shot id={`${props.who}/ledger/periods`} />

Months follow your company's time zone. A month appears once something is dated in it.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Close | No new postings without a recorded override. Earlier months must be closed, and no drafts may remain in it |
| Reopen | Postings are allowed again. Say why; it is kept on the period |
| Lock | Permanent: a locked period can never be reopened or posted into |

Each action needs a session signed in with two-step verification.

## Posting rules

<Shot id={`${props.who}/ledger/posting-rules`} />

Sales, stock and settlements post by role: cash, bank, inventory, goods received not invoiced and so on. Each row says which account a role uses. Point a role at another account and new postings follow it; journals already posted stay as they are. Changing one needs a session signed in with two-step verification.
