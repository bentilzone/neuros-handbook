The catalogue is Neuros' shared list of real products, one record per product, used by every seller. Prices and stock are not here. They live on each seller's <PageLink who={props.who} page="offers">offer</PageLink>. Two distributors selling the same pump both point at the same catalogue product, which is how a buyer can compare them side by side in the <PageLink who={props.who} page="marketplace">marketplace</PageLink>.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/catalogue/browse`} />

## Browse

- **Categories** on the left narrow the list to one branch of the tree.
- **Search** matches product name, brand and part number. **Type** filters by product type.
- **Click a row** to open the product: its category, brand, part number, GTIN, attributes, units and history.
