Customers are the buyers who hold an account with you: the requests waiting for your answer, and the terms each customer trades on. An account is a [relationship](../01-glossary/40-relationships.mdx) between two companies. Your customer sees the same account under their <PageLink who={props.who} page="suppliers">Suppliers</PageLink>.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/customers/list`} />

## Statuses

| Status | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Invited | You invited a reseller by phone; they have not sent your agent the code yet | New code, End account |
| Waiting for approval | A buyer asked for an account | Approve, with terms, or Decline |
| Approved | They trade with you on their terms | Edit terms, Suspend, End account |
| Suspended | New orders are blocked; open orders, invoices and history are untouched | Reinstate, End account |
| Declined | You said no, with a reason they see | They may ask again later |
| Ended | Trading has ended for good; existing obligations stand | They would need to ask again |

Suspend, decline and end each ask for a reason, which is kept on the account's history.

## Open an account

**Open an account** starts an approved account for a buyer who has not asked yet. Pick the **buyer** from the companies on Neuros that buy, then set their terms as above.

## Invite by phone

**Invite by phone** brings on a reseller who orders through your [AI agent](../01-glossary/45-ai-agents.mdx) and never signs in, such as a shop that orders on WhatsApp today. The number becomes a reseller account on Neuros with no users. A reseller you invite who also buys from another seller on Neuros is the same account there; each seller sees only its own business with them.

## Credit

A **credit-eligible** customer's account has a **Credit** section. Both of you see the same figures; only you change them.

{props.who === 'distributor' && <Shot id="distributor/customers/credit" />}

- **Limit:** the most the customer may owe you at once, in your currency.
- **In use:** orders on credit you accepted that are not yet paid. Each claims its value when you accept it and gives it back when it is paid or cancelled. Two orders accepted at the same moment can never both take the last of the limit.
- **Available:** the limit less what is in use.
- **Expires:** after this date, new orders cannot use credit until you renew the limit.

:::note[Credit and orders]
Accepting an order on credit claims its total; cancelling it gives the credit back. Paying it will give it back too, once invoices and payments arrive.
:::

**Set limit** (or **Change limit**) needs a sign-in made with [two-step verification](../01-glossary/10-companies-and-people.mdx#two-step-verification-mfa):

## Live

A new request, or a customer ending their account, appears on its own. Approving or suspending updates the customer's side at the same moment.

## Common questions

**A customer can't see an offer.** Offers for approved customers need the account to be **Approved**. Invite-only offers also need the customer picked on the offer.

**Why can't I set a limit?** Switch on **Credit-eligible** and allow **On credit** in the customer's terms first.

**Can a customer see my private notes or other customers' terms?** No. Each side sees only its own notes, and a customer sees only their own terms.
