You can dispute within the supplier's [dispute window](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#dispute-window), 7 days after delivery unless they set another; "not delivered" counts from dispatch.

## Follow it
{props.who === 'reseller' && <Shot id="reseller/buying-disputes/detail" />}

The supplier answers within 3 working days, or it is escalated to their administrators. Add photos, documents or a short video (up to 25 MB each) with **Add a photo or document**, and answer the supplier's questions under Messages. **Escalate** asks their administrators to step in sooner; **Withdraw** ends it, and what it held goes on.

When the supplier decides, you see the outcome and their reason:
- **Credit:** a credit note off what you owe; on a paid invoice it is held as your advance with them, on escrow that much is refunded to you.
- **Replace:** the goods are shipped again at no charge.
- **Take it back:** a return is authorised; send the goods back quoting its RMA number.
- **Reject:** nothing changes.

## Return goods
Open a delivered order and choose **Return goods**: the lines and quantities, serial numbers where the item has them, a reason and details. The supplier authorises it before you send anything, receives and inspects what arrives, and closes it with a credit note for what they accepted. The **Returns** tab follows each one: asked, authorised, received, inspected, closed or rejected.

## Live
Both tabs and an open dispute update as the supplier writes, decides, or receives your goods.

## Common questions

**Can I still pay the rest of the order?** Yes. On escrow, **Release** pays the supplier everything not under dispute.

**The window has closed.** Ask the supplier: they can extend it on your account.
