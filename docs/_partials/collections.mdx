**Collections** is how your customers pay you through Neuros’s partner bank. Each customer gets its own [virtual account](../01-glossary/47-partner-bank.mdx#virtual-account) at the bank. They pay it by ordinary transfer, with the order number as the reference. The bank tells Neuros about every [credit](../01-glossary/47-partner-bank.mdx#bank-credit) at once, and Neuros puts it against the order it pays. An escrow order is held by the bank until the [dispute window](../01-glossary/47-partner-bank.mdx#dispute-window) after delivery has passed. See [Collections and escrow](../02-features/47-collections-and-escrow.mdx) for the whole flow.

## Credits

Every payment into your customers’ accounts, newest first: who paid, how much, the order it paid and what became of it.

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| Matched | It paid the order in full. An escrow order is now funded, held by the bank |
| Short | It paid less than the order needs. The customer is told in chat what remains; the next payment adds to it |
| More than due | It paid more. On an escrow order the bank sends the excess back to the payer; on another order it is kept as the customer’s advance |
| To match | Neuros could not tell which order it pays. It waits for you |
| Returned | You sent it back to the payer |

Neuros finds the order by the order number in the payment’s narration. With no number, it takes the customer’s only accepted, unpaid order of exactly that amount. A credit for an order you have not accepted yet stays **To match** until you accept the order and match it.

Open a credit to see its narration, who paid, the bank’s reference, and why it matched or did not.

{props.who === 'distributor' && <Shot id={`${props.who}/collections/credit`} />}

- **Match to an order**: choose one of that customer’s accepted, unpaid orders. An escrow order collects the money, and is funded once it is covered. Any other order gets a receipt for it, as if you had confirmed the transfer yourself. Needs `payment:initiate`.
- **Send back**: the bank returns the money to the account it came from, with your reason. It cannot be undone. Needs `payment:approve` and a session signed in with two-step verification.

## Accounts

Your customers’ virtual accounts: the account number, the name the bank shows to a payer, and the bank’s identity checks on the holder.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/collections/accounts`} />

While collection is on, approving a customer opens its account. A customer approved earlier gets one when you click **Open one** in its panel under <PageLink who={props.who} page="customers">Customers</PageLink>, or when your AI agent first gives them payment instructions. The customer sees the same account on their supplier page and on each unpaid order.

## Reconciliation

Each night Neuros compares the day’s statement from the bank with what it recorded. Each day shows **Agreed**, or **Differences** to look at.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/collections/reconciliation`} />

| Difference | Means | What Neuros did |
|---|---|---|
| Taken in from the statement | The bank shows a payment its webhook never reported | Recorded it and matched it like any other credit |
| Not on the bank’s statement | Neuros recorded a credit the bank does not show | Nothing: ask the bank |
| Paid out, not asked by Neuros | Money left an account without a return from Neuros | Nothing: ask the bank |
| Return not on the statement | A return the bank confirmed is not on its statement | Nothing: ask the bank |

**Reconcile a day** runs one day again now; its result replaces the earlier one.

The partner bank takes the platform’s fee from each escrow release, and you receive the rest. The order shows the fee, and your books record it as an expense (**Platform fees**). Changing these settings needs `bank_account:manage` and a session signed in with two-step verification.
