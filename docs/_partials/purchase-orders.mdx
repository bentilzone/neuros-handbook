## Approve and send
**Send to supplier** issues it:

- **Over your purchase limit**, it needs approving first, unless it came from an approved requisition. Someone who approves purchase orders, other than who drafted it, clicks **Approve**, within their approval limit. Editing an approved draft withdraws the approval.
- **Sent**, it is emailed to the supplier’s order email with every line and the total, and the supplier’s details are kept as they were when it was sent. The PDF (the icon at the top of the panel) is the same order, to send another way when the supplier has no email.

## Confirmation and cancelling
When the supplier confirms, click **Supplier confirmed** and record their reference and the delivery date they promised. **Cancel** takes a reason and is possible only while nothing has been received against the order. If it was already sent, tell the supplier yourself.

## Live
The list and an open purchase order update as your team approves, sends and confirms them.

## Common questions

**Does the supplier get a login?** No. External suppliers are not on Neuros: they receive the email and the PDF, and confirm to you directly.

**Can I order from a seller on Neuros here?** No, order from them under Orders or the Marketplace, so they receive it in Neuros.
