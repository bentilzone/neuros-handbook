## Match and approve
**Match** checks each line against the order’s price and what was received and not yet invoiced:

- **Matched**: the price is within your company’s price tolerance (2% unless changed in Settings) and the quantity within its quantity tolerance (exact unless changed).
- **Price differs**, **Quantity differs**, **Not matched**: the invoice is an **exception**, and the people who approve supplier invoices are notified.

A matched invoice is approved with **Approve for payment**. An exception is approved with **Approve anyway** and a reason, by someone other than who recorded it; the reason stays with the invoice. Approving records what you owe; a price difference adjusts the cost of the goods still on hand, and of cost of sales for what has already gone.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/payables/detail`} />

**Void** takes a reason and is possible only before approval. After approval, the supplier corrects an invoice with a credit note.

## Record a supplier’s credit note
When a supplier takes money off an approved invoice (a price corrected, goods sent back, damaged or short), open the invoice and choose **Record a credit note**. It needs supplier_invoice:approve.

The credit comes off what you still owe on the invoice. If the invoice was already paid, the rest is taken back off its payment and held as an **advance** with the supplier, which you apply to its next invoice from the payment (**Apply the advance**). In the books it posts at the invoice’s own exchange rate: what you owe goes down, the input tax comes back off, and the cost of the goods the invoice billed goes down (the stock still on hand, then cost of sales for what has gone; freight for a charges invoice). A credit note cannot be voided.

## Pay
**Pay a supplier** on the **Payments** tab either records a payment already made (**Already paid**), or schedules one for a date (**Schedule it**), which someone who approves payments marks paid when it goes out.

**Void** on a payment takes a reason; a paid one is reversed in the books and its invoices are owed again.

## Ageing
The **Ageing** tab shows what you owe each supplier, by how long past the due date: not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90 days.

## Live
Invoices, payments and the ageing update as your team records, matches, approves and pays.

## Common questions

**Why can’t I void an approved invoice?** It is already in the books and may be paid. Supplier credit notes are not in Neuros yet; record the correction with your supplier and a manual journal.

**Does a seller on Neuros see my payment?** A payment here is your own record. Tell the seller you paid on the order, under <PageLink who={props.who} page="buying-orders">Orders</PageLink>, so they can confirm it.
