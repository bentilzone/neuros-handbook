Fulfilment is where accepted orders leave your warehouse. Each delivery is a [shipment](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#shipment): you pick it, pack it, dispatch it and prove it arrived. One order can go out in several shipments, from one warehouse each.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/fulfilment/list`} />

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Picking | Started; the stock is still reserved, not taken |
| Packed | What went in the box is confirmed |
| On its way | Dispatched: the stock has left inventory |
| Delivered | Delivered, with proof |
| Cancelled | Stopped before it left; its stock stays reserved for the order |

## Start a shipment

Open an accepted order under <PageLink who={props.who} page="orders">Orders</PageLink> and choose **Start a shipment**. It takes everything reserved for the order in one warehouse that is not already in another shipment. Backordered stock is not shipped until it is reserved. While a shipment is open the order cannot be changed.

## Pick

The **Pick list** tab shows, for one warehouse, every shipment being picked: each item, how much, the bins its stock is reserved in, and for serialised products the serial numbers reserved. See [pick list](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#pick-list).

## Prove delivery

**Add proof** attaches the signed delivery note or a photo (PDF, PNG or JPEG, up to 10 MB). **Delivered** needs at least one; see [delivery proof](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#delivery-proof). When everything ordered has been dispatched and delivered, the order's stock status is *Delivered*.

**Delivery failed** records why it did not arrive (customer closed, wrong address) and puts it back to *Packed*, ready to dispatch again. The customer sees the reason. See [failed delivery](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#failed-delivery).

**Cancel shipment** stops one that has not left. Its stock stays reserved for the order.

The delivery note PDF opens from the shipment's header. Every step needs `shipment:manage`; adding proof also needs `document:upload`.

## Returns

{props.who === 'distributor' && <Shot id="distributor/fulfilment/returns" />}

The **Returns** tab lists returns still open: authorised ones to receive when the goods arrive, and received ones to inspect. Opening one gives the same steps as under <PageLink who={props.who} page="disputes">Disputes and returns</PageLink>: **Receive** brings the goods into quarantine in a warehouse, not free to sell; **Inspect** puts each line back on sale, into damaged stock, aside, or back to its supplier. The tab shows to people who can see returns.

## Live

A colleague packing or dispatching, and the customer confirming receipt, appear on their own.

## Common questions

**Why is a line not in the shipment?** Its stock is backordered, or already in another open shipment.

**Why can't I cancel a shipment that failed?** Its stock has already left inventory. Dispatch it again, or agree a return with the customer.
