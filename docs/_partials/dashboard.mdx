The first page after you sign in. It names the company you are working in and, as trading arrives, the money settled, locked and disputed across your orders.

## What is on it

- **The company you are in.** People who work for more than one company switch here. Everything you see and do applies to this company only. See [companies and people](../01-glossary/10-companies-and-people.mdx).
- **The sidebar.** Grouped into Main, Sell, Buy, Stock & finance and System. A distributor sees all of them. A supplier has no Buy section and a reseller has no Sell or Inventory. Pages you lack the permission for are hidden, not greyed out.
- **Live.** The green dot means your browser is receiving live updates. Lists across the app refresh on their own when a colleague, customer or supplier changes something. If it goes grey, reload the page.
- **Settled, Locked, Disputed.** These fill in once orders and settlement arrive. Until then they show a dash.

## Common questions

**Why don't I see a page a colleague sees?** Pages follow your roles. Ask a company administrator to check yours under <PageLink who={props.who} page="members">Members</PageLink>.
