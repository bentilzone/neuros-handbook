Suppliers are the companies you buy from. **On Neuros** lists the sellers you have accounts with: the terms each one gave you, and your requests for new accounts. It is the buyer's side of the same account your supplier sees under their <PageLink who={props.who} page="customers">Customers</PageLink>. **External** lists the suppliers you buy from outside Neuros.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/suppliers/list`} />

## Find suppliers

**Find suppliers** searches the sellers on Neuros by name. You see public business identity only: name, country and what kind of company they are. No prices, stock or contacts. Each one also shows your account with them, if you have one. Ask for an account from the <PageLink who={props.who} page="marketplace">marketplace</PageLink> offer or from here.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/suppliers/find`} />

## A supplier account

Click a row for the account: its status, **your terms** as the supplier set them, your own reference for them, and the history.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/suppliers/detail`} />

<FieldTable fields={[
  { name: 'Supplier code', meaning: 'Your own code for this supplier, for example the one in your purchasing system.', others: 'Only your company sees it. The supplier never does.' },
]} />

**Your terms** are read-only here: how you usually pay, the ways you may pay, credit, the largest order, and when the account runs. Only the supplier changes them.

When the supplier offers you credit, a **Credit** section shows your **limit**, what is **in use** (orders on credit accepted and not yet paid), what is **available**, and when it **expires**. If the supplier puts your account **on hold**, it says so with their reason: orders on credit wait until they lift it.

**End account** ends trading with the supplier. Existing obligations stand. To trade again you would ask for a new account.

| Status | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Waiting for approval | Your request is with the supplier |
| Approved | You can order on your terms |
| Suspended | The supplier blocked new orders; open orders continue |
| Declined | The supplier said no, with a reason; you may ask again later |
| Ended | Trading has ended |

## Suppliers outside Neuros

See [external supplier](../01-glossary/40-relationships.mdx#external-supplier). A seller on Neuros is never added here: it is your account with them, on the other tab.

**Deactivate** stops new orders to a supplier you no longer use; its orders and invoices stay. **Reactivate** brings it back.

### Where it is paid

A supplier's bank details are kept apart from the rest. Only people who may manage bank accounts (`bank_account:manage`) change them, after signing in with two-step verification, and with a reason that goes into the audit log. Everyone else sees the last four digits of the account number. See [supplier bank details](../01-glossary/40-relationships.mdx#supplier-bank-details).

:::warning[Confirm every change]
A message asking you to pay a supplier into a new account is the most common way payments are stolen. Before saving new bank details, call the supplier on a number you already had, not one in the message.
:::
