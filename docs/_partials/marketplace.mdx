## Search and filter

- **Search** matches products, brands, part numbers and sellers. **Sort** by best match, newest, or name.
- **Filters** on the left (availability, category, brand, seller) show how many offers each would leave. **Clear filters** resets them.
- **A card** shows the product, the seller and their country, the price per unit, and availability: In stock, Low stock, On backorder or Made to order.
- **Price for you** in place of a price means the seller prices you individually. Open the offer and price it.

## An offer

Click a card to open the offer: product facts, the seller's SKU, minimum order, units, where they deliver, and how they supply it.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/marketplace/offer`} />

- **Price it** works out your price for a quantity from your account terms with this seller: their contract with you, your price list, quantity breaks and any promotion. The answer is indicative until an order or quotation locks it.
- **Ask for an account** appears when you have no account with the seller. It sends them your request.
- **Add to order** appears when you have an account and may place orders. It puts the offer, at the quantity and unit you chose, into the order you are building with that seller: your open draft with them, or a new draft if there is none. Adding the same offer again adds to its line. The draft is priced at your price now and stays yours until you submit it from <PageLink who={props.who} page="buying-orders">Orders</PageLink>; **Open order** takes you there.
- **Request a quote** appears when you have an account. It asks the seller to quote this offer for a quantity; the request and their answer are under <PageLink who={props.who} page="buying-quotes">Quotes</PageLink>.

## Live

When a seller publishes, changes or withdraws an offer, the marketplace updates on its own. The same happens when your account with them is approved.

## Common questions

**Which order does Add to order use?** Your company's open draft with that seller, other than one placed from a quotation (a quotation's order keeps the quotation's lines). With none, it starts a draft. One order is always with one seller, so offers from two sellers make two drafts.

**Why do I see an offer one colleague says is missing?** Everyone in your company sees the same marketplace. Check the filters, and whether the offer is invite-only for another buyer.
