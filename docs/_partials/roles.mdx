A role is a named set of permissions. Members get what their roles allow. See [people and access](../02-features/20-people-and-access.mdx).

<Shot id={`${props.who}/roles/list`} />

- **System roles** follow Neuros' templates, such as Company administrator, Finance approver or Warehouse operator. They stay in step with new features, and you cannot edit them.
- **Custom roles** are yours. **Duplicate** a system role to start one, then change it.

## A role

Click a role for its permissions, grouped by area. Each chip is one action: view, manage, approve and so on.

<Shot id={`${props.who}/roles/detail`} />

## New or edited role

Creating or editing a role needs a session signed in with two-step verification.
