**Imports** bring records in from a CSV file instead of one form at a time: your offers, products for the catalogue, the stock you held when you started, your suppliers, resellers to invite by phone, or prices on a price list. Every row is checked against the same rules as the form before anything goes in. You confirm, and each row goes in whole or not at all. See [import](../01-glossary/85-integrations.mdx#import).

Your plan must include Bulk import. At most 10,000 rows per file.

:::note[Not in your plan]
Without it, **Imports** shows a padlock in the sidebar. The page still opens with your earlier imports and their rows, but **Import from a file** is off until Bulk import is added under **Settings → Billing**.
:::

## What you can import

| Kind | Needs | Each row becomes |
|---|---|---|
| Offers | A company that sells, offer:manage | An offer, as a draft or published |
| Products | catalogue:contribute | A product contribution, sent to Neuros for review |
| Opening stock | A company that holds stock, stock:receive | Stock received into a warehouse at its cost, against opening balance equity |
| Suppliers | A company that buys, supplier:manage | An external supplier. Its bank details are added on the supplier afterwards |
| Resellers by phone | A company that sells, relationship:manage | A reseller invited by phone to order through your AI agent, with its terms |
| Price list prices | A company that sells, pricelist:manage | An offer’s prices on a price list, replacing any it had there |

## Import a file

<Shot id="distributor/imports/new" />

1. Choose the kind and **Download the template**: the columns, then one example row in your company’s currency. The table under it says what each column means; the starred ones are required.
2. Fill in one row per record. Keep the header row.
3. Choose the file and **Upload and check**. Nothing is imported yet.

### Offer columns

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| `product` * | The product’s Neuros code (NEU-PROD-…) or its GTIN; it must be published in the catalogue |
| `sku` * | Your own code for the offer, unique among your offers |
| `title` | The name buyers see; the product’s name if empty |
| `price` * | Price per unit, before tax |
| `currency` | Three-letter code; your company’s currency if empty |
| `unit` | The unit the price is per; the product’s base unit if empty |
| `min_order_qty`, `order_increment` | The least a buyer may order, and the multiple orders must be in |
| `lead_time_days` | Days from order to dispatch |
| `visibility` | `public`, `relationship` (your customers only) or `invite_only`; `relationship` if empty |

### Product columns

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| `name` * | The product’s name, as the maker gives it |
| `category` * | The category’s name, exactly as in the catalogue |
| `base_unit` * | The unit stock is counted in |
| `brand`, `manufacturer`, `mpn` | The brand, who makes it, and the maker’s part number |
| `gtin` | GTIN-8, 12, 13 or 14 (the barcode number) |
| `weight_kg`, `description` | Shipping weight in kilograms; what it is, for buyers |

### Opening stock columns

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| `product` * | The product’s Neuros code or its GTIN |
| `warehouse` * | Your warehouse’s code |
| `quantity` * | How many you hold, in the unit given |
| `unit` | The unit counted in; the product’s base unit if empty |
| `unit_cost` * | What each cost you, in your company’s currency; sets the stock’s value in the books |
| `lot_number`, `expires_at` | For a lot-controlled product, and its expiry date (YYYY-MM-DD) |
| `serial_numbers` | For a serial-numbered product: one per unit, separated by `|` |

### Supplier columns

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| `code` * | Your own short code for the supplier: letters, digits, dot, dash or underscore; unique in your company |
| `name` * | The name you know it by |
| `legal_name`, `tax_id` | Its registered name and tax number, for documents |
| `country`, `email`, `phone` | Two-letter country code; where purchase orders are emailed; its phone |
| `currency` | The currency its prices and invoices come in; your company’s currency if empty |
| `payment_terms_days` | Days after its invoice that payment is due; 30 if empty |
| `lead_time_days`, `notes` | Usual days from order to delivery; anything worth keeping |

### Reseller columns

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| `phone` * | The shop’s number, with its country code or as a local number in your country. One shop per number across Neuros |
| `name` * | The shop’s business name |
| `channels` | `whatsapp`, `sms`, or both separated by `|`; both if empty |
| `price_list` | The name of your price list it buys from |
| `payment_mode`, `terms_days` | How it pays by default, and days to pay for credit |
| `tier`, `zone`, `external_ref` | Your grouping of it, its delivery zone, and its code in your own books |

Each row is an invitation by phone, as **Invite by phone** makes on Customers. The invitation code is not shown in an import: when your messages are on it goes to the reseller’s chat, otherwise give them a new code from their account.

### Price list price columns

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| `price_list` * | The price list’s name, exactly as you named it |
| `sku` * | Your offer’s SKU; the offer must be in the price list’s currency |
| `unit` | The unit the price is per; the offer’s unit if empty |
| `unit_price` * | Price per unit before tax, from a quantity of 1 |
| `quantity_breaks` | Lower prices from a quantity up, as `quantity=price` separated by `|`, e.g. `10=178000.00|50=175000.00` |
| `valid_from`, `valid_to` | The first and last day the prices apply (YYYY-MM-DD) |

## Check and confirm

<Shot id="distributor/imports/detail" />

The import shows how many rows are **Ready** and how many are **To fix**, with what is wrong with each. **Problems file** downloads the rows to fix as you wrote them, with a `problems` column. Correct them and import that file afterwards.

**Import _n_ rows** imports the ready rows in the background; the rows to fix are left out. The counts update as they go in. A row that fails at that point, for example because a limit was reached in the meantime, is **Not imported**, with the reason, and is in the problems file too.

**Cancel import** ends an import that has not been confirmed. Nothing from it is imported.

## Common questions

**Can I undo an import?** Not as a whole. Imported offers can be withdrawn, and opening stock corrected with a stock adjustment.

**Who is recorded as having made the records?** The person who confirmed the import, with the permissions they held then.
