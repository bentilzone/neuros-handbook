Service cases is your inbox for whatever a customer raises with you: a payment not showing, a late delivery, goods that are not right, a question about their account. Customers open cases in the app, or in chat through your [AI agent](../01-glossary/45-ai-agents.mdx#ai-agent). Neuros opens one itself when a [delivery code locks](../01-glossary/48-deliveries.mdx#locked-code) at the door. See [service case](../01-glossary/49-service-cases.mdx#service-case) and [Service cases](../02-features/49-service-cases.mdx).

<Shot id={`${props.who}/cases/list`} />

The list shows open cases unless you choose a state. **Overdue** shows the cases past one of your times. **Mine** shows the cases given to you. A lock means the case holds the order's escrow.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| To answer | With you |
| Waiting on the customer | You wrote to them; it comes back when they answer |
| Resolved | Ended by you, by Neuros from its records, or with its dispute |
| Withdrawn | The customer withdrew it |

## Work a case

{props.who === 'distributor' && <Shot id="distributor/cases/detail" />}

A case shows what it is about, the order and delivery it names, how it was opened, who has it, your two times, and the messages.

- **Write** to the customer. Your first message stops the clock for a first answer, and puts the case to *Waiting on the customer*. A customer who orders by chat gets your message in their chat too.
- **Take it** gives the case to you. Someone who can see your company's people can give it to a colleague who answers cases, or back to nobody. Whoever gets it is told.
- **Resolve** ends it, saying what was done. The customer reads it, in their chat too when they order by chat.

A **goods** case was opened with a [dispute](../01-glossary/30-selling-and-pricing.mdx#dispute) on its lines. Decide the dispute under <PageLink who={props.who} page="disputes">Disputes and returns</PageLink>. The case resolves with it, so it has no **Resolve** of its own.

## Escrow while a case is open

A payment, delivery or other case on an escrow order holds the whole escrow until the case ends. Nothing is released to you meanwhile: not by the buyer, and not when the [dispute window](../01-glossary/47-partner-bank.mdx#dispute-window) passes. When it ends, anything the buyer already released goes to you at once. A goods case's dispute holds only its lines' value.

## Resolved from the records

Neuros resolves a **payment** case once the order is paid in full or its escrow is funded, and a **delivery** case once the delivery is made. It tells the customer why. Turn this off under **Settings**.

## Your times

**Settings** holds, for each type, the hours you take to give a first answer and to resolve. A case past either time shows as overdue, and the people who answer cases are told once for each.

A customer's new case or answer, a colleague's reply and a case resolved from the records appear on their own.
