Invoices lists what your suppliers have billed you for: what you owe each one, when it is due, and what you have paid. A supplier invoices each delivery as it leaves, or a whole order when they accept it.

Open an invoice for its lines, the delivery and order it is for, its due date, and **Settled by**: each payment the supplier recorded and each credit note they issued. The PDF opens from its header. An invoice past its due date with something outstanding shows **Overdue**.

You pay outside Neuros, by the arrangement on your account. The supplier records your payment, and the invoice shows it as part paid or paid. Your order's **Payment** status follows its invoices.

Reading needs `bill:read`.

## Common questions

**I paid but the invoice still shows unpaid.** The supplier has not recorded it yet. Send them your transfer reference.

**Why is the amount less than my order?** It is one delivery of it. The rest is invoiced as it ships.
