Pricing is where you give customers their own prices: **price lists** for groups of customers, **contract prices** for one customer and one offer, and **promotions** for a while. The **price check** shows which one a customer gets, and why.

## How a customer's price is chosen

For a customer, an offer and a quantity, Neuros takes the first of these that applies:

1. **A contract price** agreed with that customer for that offer.
2. **The customer's price list**, the one named in their <PageLink who={props.who} page="customers">terms</PageLink>, if the offer is on it.
3. **The offer's standard price**, with its quantity breaks.

Then **promotions** apply, except on a contract price. A contract is a price you agreed, so a sale never discounts it further.

The price a buyer sees in the marketplace is indicative. An order or quotation locks it.

## Price lists

<Shot id={`${props.who}/pricing/price-lists`} />

A price list is a named set of prices, for example "Gold resellers". It does nothing until a customer's terms name it: open the customer under **Customers** and set **Price list** in their terms. Offers not on the list still sell to that customer at your standard price.

### A promotion

<Shot id={`${props.who}/pricing/promotion-detail`} />

Click a row to open it:
- its terms in words, who gets it and when it runs;
- what its budget has given (orders and discount, against each cap);
- the orders it went on, marked *Given back* once an order was rejected or cancelled.

A running promotion can be **paused**, **resumed** or **ended**. An ended promotion cannot change.

**Tell resellers** sends it to your resellers' chat, as a marketing message, for those who qualify, order through your AI agent and agreed to marketing. Each gets it once, and none more than one marketing message from you a week. See <PageLink who={props.who} page="messages">Messages</PageLink>.

## Price check

<Shot id={`${props.who}/pricing/check`} />

Pick an offer, a customer and a quantity, then press **Check price**. You see the price they would pay and each step that produced it: contract, list, breaks, promotions. Leave the customer empty to see the public price. Use it before you answer "why did I get this price?".

## Live

Prices you change apply at once to new price checks and orders. A colleague's new list, contract or promotion appears in your tabs without reloading.
