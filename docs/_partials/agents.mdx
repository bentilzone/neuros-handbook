An **AI agent** takes your resellers’ orders where they already talk to you: WhatsApp, SMS and email. The agent runs on an agent platform you choose and holds the conversation. Neuros gives it every fact and does every action: the reseller’s own price, the promotions they qualify for, the total, how to pay, where the order is. An agent never works out a figure itself. See [Selling through AI agents](../02-features/45-selling-through-agents.mdx) for the whole flow.

## Agents

Each agent is listed with the numbers it answers on, its calls and refused calls in the last 30 days, and when it was last used.

**Add an agent** needs two-step verification:

<FieldTable fields={[
  { name: 'Name', required: true, meaning: 'How your team knows the agent.', others: 'Resellers never see it. Orders it places show it in their history.' },
  { name: 'What it may do', required: true, meaning: 'The tools the agent may call (see below).', implications: 'A tool not ticked is refused, and the refusal is counted. Untick Place orders to let an agent quote but not order.', rules: 'At least one.' },
  { name: 'Answers on', meaning: 'Your numbers and addresses this agent speaks for, from the Numbers tab.', rules: 'Only active numbers.' },
]} />

When you save it, Neuros shows the agent’s **client ID** and **secret** once. Put both in your agent platform; it exchanges them for a token and calls the tools with it. If the secret is lost, **rotate** it: the old one stops working at once. **Switch off** ends the agent for good.

### What an agent may do

| Tool | What it does for the reseller |
|---|---|
| Know who is writing | Recognises the number as one of your customers, an invited reseller, or a stranger, and greets them |
| Accept invitations | Accepts your invitation when the reseller sends the code |
| Find products and prices | Finds your products from how the reseller describes them, at their own price |
| Quote a cart | Prices the cart with their terms, promotions (free goods and order discounts included) and tax, and holds the prices for 30 minutes. Says each offer applied and what it saves, and how much more would reach the next order discount |
| Place orders | Places the quoted cart once the reseller says yes |
| Order status | Says where an order is |
| How to pay | Gives the amount, the account and the reference |
| Delivery status | Says whether it has left, with whom, and when it arrived |
| Statement and credit | Says what they owe you, what is overdue and the credit left |

An agent sees only your side: it never finds what a reseller buys from another seller, even one that uses Neuros and the same reseller.

## Numbers

The **Numbers** tab lists your WhatsApp numbers, SMS senders and email addresses. **Add a number** with its country code, or a local number in your country (Neuros writes it in full). A number belongs to one company on Neuros. Switch a number off to stop agents answering on it without removing it.

## Resellers for your agent

Your agent answers customers whose numbers it knows. On <PageLink who={props.who} page="customers">Customers</PageLink>:

- **Invite by phone** brings on a shop that orders by chat and never signs in. You get a six-digit code to send them; they send it to your agent to accept.
- On any customer’s account, **Your agent → Edit** sets the numbers they write from, their tier and zone, and your code for them.

Orders an agent places arrive on <PageLink who={props.who} page="orders">Orders</PageLink> like any other, marked **By chat**.
